Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one high-resolution landscape ART-DIRECTION CONCEPT SHEET for an original fictional video-game assault rifle named VANDAL. This is a static visual proposal for a professional stylised hero-shooter game asset. Present the complete exterior and enlarged exterior design details, without manufacturing dimensions or assembly instructions.

Layout: a large, polished FRONT/SIDE THREE-QUARTER hero view occupying the upper left two-thirds, with the entire rifle visible and the front face of the muzzle visible. Below it, a full clean side view of the EXACT SAME design. On the right, four generous detail panels: MAGAZINE FIT; RECEIVER; SIGHTS (include a small eye-level ADS inset); CHARM MOUNT. Keep the design completely consistent between views. Title only "VANDAL / DIRECTION 01", with these short panel labels in understated typography. Light neutral grey studio backdrop, soft broad key light and gentle rim light, exposure that reveals the actual dark colours and corner geometry.

Art direction: distinctly more crafted, characterful and mechanically integrated than a simple low-poly blockout. Original design, no copied Valorant, RIVALS or real-world weapon silhouette. Aim for the deliberate surface craft of a premium hero-shooter asset and a clear, substantial first-person silhouette. Sculpted receiver with stepped sections and broad designed shoulders; coherent transition into a tapered handguard with deep inset vents and neatly recessed fasteners; substantial ergonomic grip with fine moulded grip pattern; a multi-plane shoulder stock and cheek piece with an integrated connection to the receiver. Strong primary shapes, purposeful secondary assemblies, restrained small detail. Precise fitted seams and recesses. Carefully shaped corners with broad purposeful chamfers and smaller edge breaks appropriate to each piece. Every component should feel designed together; avoid a long rectangular slab, plain primitive parts, arbitrary decorative strips and a generic triangular cut-out stock.

The magazine is a central design feature: elegant moderate curvature, deliberate front/back spine, shaped side recesses, subtle ribs, and a finished chamfered baseplate. Its narrowed upper section is visibly INSERTED AND SEATED inside a closely fitting magazine well with a coherent lip and catch. Show the complete magazine silhouette alongside a close-up of the seated junction in the MAGAZINE FIT panel. No gaps, floating magazine, unrelated box shapes or loose cartridges perched outside the receiver.

The RECEIVER panel shows the exterior charging handle integrated into its recessed track and the carefully framed bolt/ejection area. Small gripping surfaces, nested forms, crisp corner treatment, intentional material boundaries; no floating cuboid handle or pasted-on metal rectangle.
The SIGHTS panel shows one compact low-profile enclosed reflex optic with a fine rounded rectangular housing, clear lens and slim supporting base integrated into the upper receiver. The eye-level inset shows a clear target opening and tiny reticle. No oversized iron-sight towers, bare tall posts, arbitrary riser block or view-obstructing frame.
The CHARM MOUNT panel shows a small rounded D-shaped eyelet supported by a recessed mounting base on the rear receiver. Make the connection and clearance legible; it is a finished attachment built into the weapon, not a floating square ring. No charm character is needed.

Materials: clean dark graphite coated body, restrained cool slate secondary surfaces, satin dark steel on exposed working surfaces, subtly textured dark polymer grip and magazine, one very small cyan status inset and a tiny warm metal control. Rich material separation and controlled fine roughness, tasteful edge highlights. The colour should come from considered material choices rather than large unrelated blue and gold slabs. No grime, stains, random scratches, cloudy procedural noise, glowing tubes, excessive greebles, real brands, logos or text on the rifle. Give the sheet the quality and intentionality of a senior game weapon artist's design review.
