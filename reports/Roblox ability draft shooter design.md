# Draft Every Duel, Then Crown a Gauntlet

The design that fits the research best is a RIVALS-shaped game with ability drafting added to every queue. That means a social lobby, quick queues from 1v1 up to 4v4, short elimination rounds and a first-to-5 win condition. The drafting itself is not one 16-player mode bolted on. Each player starts a match with one drafted ability, then drafts again after every round. Picks either add an ability (up to three) or level an owned one from L1 to L3, with a branch at L2. Rare "glitch" variants appear as visible, optional draft cards, not as surprises in the middle of a fight. The Gauntlet becomes a scheduled tournament event. It runs 8 duos in one reserved server with four parallel arenas and a shared team health pool, and players keep playing duels while its queue fills. This structure matters because the evidence points two ways. RIVALS became Roblox's top shooter with crisp gunplay and a simple, clip-friendly duel format ([Rolimons: RIVALS](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17625359962)). The closest Roblox draft shooter, OVERKILL, peaked around 7K CCU and then fell to roughly 500 ([profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/overkill)). So the draft has to sit on top of excellent gunplay and fast queues, not replace them. On the technical side, Roblox in October 2026 supports this with MemoryStore-based queues, reserved servers, the native Party API, the generally available Server Authority model and an official aim-assist module. Two constraints shape the rest of the design: a small early player base, and 2026 platform rules on paid random items, chat age checks and recommendation signals that reward retention and co-play. Those constraints drive the MVP below: Duels and Duos first, then 1v1 Ranked, Squads and the Gauntlet, each added once metrics show it is ready.

## The evidence base is wide but mostly secondhand

Every research stream hit the same limit, and readers should weigh the findings with it in mind. The research environment blocked direct page fetches for nearly all external sites, including devforum.roblox.com, create.roblox.com and most Valorant and RIVALS coverage. As a result, **almost every community, press and DevForum claim in this document comes from search-engine result summaries, not full-page reads.** Roblox's official documentation is the main exception. It was read in full from the Roblox/creator-docs GitHub repository (the source of create.roblox.com) at its 2026-10-16 commit, so API names, quotas and limits are the highest-confidence facts here ([Roblox docs: Memory stores](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud-services/memory-stores/index.md)). The open-source matchmaking modules were cloned and read directly.

Several facts conflict across sources, and the report flags them where they come up. Player-count trackers disagree on RIVALS' all-time peak by up to about 40% (674K to 967K) and on Arsenal's by roughly 3x. RIVALS' ranked unlock requirements differ between guides, from Level 30 to Level 100. Gauntlet: Glitched's 100 HP pool and 40-second rounds each rest on one or two guide sources. Overwatch Stadium's mercy rule is described two different ways. Roblox's own security docs still call Server Authority "beta" even though it has been fully released. Throughout, paragraphs marked **Evidence** report sourced findings, and paragraphs marked **Recommendation** give this document's design judgment. Specific numbers in recommendations (timers, odds, HP values) are starting points for playtesting, not sourced facts.

## RIVALS won on format and feel, while draft shooters stayed niche

**Evidence.** RIVALS (Nosniy Games, mid-2024) is the model to study. It is a social lobby where players step on duel pads that queue team sizes from 1v1 to 5v5. Matches go to the first side to win 5 rounds, and each player carries a four-slot loadout (primary, secondary, melee, utility) ([bloxodes gamemodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/gamemodes); [bloxodes beginner guide](https://bloxodes.com/articles/rivals-beginner-guide)). Trackers put its all-time peak between **about 675K and 967K CCU (Feb 2026)**, with a typical 2026 range of 110K–290K ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17625359962); [RoMonitor](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/17625359962/); [RoWatcher Mar 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown)). It won the RIA25 Best Shooter award. According to its developers, they chose duels because duels were the fastest thing to build ([Rivals Fandom: Nosniy Games](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Nosniy_Games)). Reviewers praise gunplay that is "crisp, responsive, and demands actual skill" ([Sportskeeda Oct 2026](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/best-roblox-games-to-play-right-now)). Content creators fuel growth with videos such as 1v1s against the owner ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUldYVN0QF8)).

The lobby uses both in-server and cross-server play. Duel Zone pads match players inside the same server and run the fight in arenas within that lobby. A global "Play" button, added in July 2024, matches players across servers into private servers and was described as placing players "almost instantly" (search summary: [Sportskeeda: Rivals Update 2](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/news-roblox-rivals-update-2-global-matchmaking-new-charms-balance-changes); [RIVALS wiki: Lobby](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Lobby)). Ranked has several friction gates. Players need a minimum level, a set number of duel wins and an account at least 14 days old, then play 10 placement matches. Ranked caps loadout swaps at 2 per duel. Parties must be within 800 Elo of each other. Leaving a ranked match gives a 15-minute lockout and an automatic loss ([Sportskeeda ranked guide](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/rivals-ranked-guide); [RIVALS wiki: Ranked](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranked)). Exact thresholds conflict between sources (Level 30, 50 or 100, sometimes with ID verification), which suggests they have been tightened over time ([bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/gamemodes); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/6035872082)).

The other big shooters each excel at one thing, and all are now far smaller. Arsenal fell from **152.6K CCU (2020) to about 2.7K (2026)**, and players complained about slow bug fixes and good maps moved behind VIP servers ([Rolimons: Arsenal](https://www.rolimons.com/game/286090429); [Arsenal Fandom: why people quit](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000228832)). Bad Business fell from 33.7K to about 144. Its developers blamed a 2023 change to Roblox's recommendation algorithm that cut new-player inflow, not a loss of interest ([DevForum: algorithm changes are killing our game](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-algorithm-changes-are-killing-our-game/2417236)). Frontlines showed that "AAA" visual fidelity alone produced a cult following of about 1K daily players, not a hit ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/news-kreekcraft-shares-call-duty-style-fps-made-roblox-leaves-players-disbelief)). Hypershot, now the number-two shooter, adds **one active ability per match** to arena movement. It peaked at 101K CCU and held about 22K in October 2026 ([newgame.co.jp](https://newgame.co.jp/metaverse/en/roblox/17516596118/hypershot/guide/); [Rolimons: Hypershot](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17516596118); [RoCodes](https://rocodes.gg/codes/hypershot)).

The closest existing Roblox games are small. OVERKILL is a ROUNDS-style shooter where players pick one of three upgrade cards and only the round loser gets upgrades. It surged to about 7K CCU and sat near 474 by September 2026 ([Deltia's Gaming](https://deltiasgaming.com/roblox-overkill-a-beginners-guide/); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/overkill-hits-7k-players-a-4-6-surge-worth-watching)). A different listing with the same name shows a 15K peak, so the identity of these figures is uncertain ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/8420998291/overkill)). No Valorant-style drafting shooter on Roblox has public player counts.

Player complaints follow familiar patterns. Aimbot and ESP scripts for RIVALS are sold openly ([itch.io listing](https://itch.io/blog/1363626/rivals-script-keyless)). Device type can only be detected on the client, so PC players can spoof mobile and pick up mobile-only aim assist ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-stop-pc-players-from-accessing-aim-assisted-guns-meant-for-mobile-game-security/2019942)). DevForum developers advise giving mobile players helper *features* rather than damage or HP buffs ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/should-i-give-mobileconsole-players-a-advantage/2051468)). The audience is young and mostly on phones. About **73% of age-checked users are under 18** ([SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/)), and Roblox's own docs state that "the majority of Roblox sessions are played on mobile devices" ([Roblox Docs: Mobile input](https://create.roblox.com/docs/input/mobile)).

**Recommendation.** Treat these as five design rules. First, gunplay quality is non-negotiable and comes before ability content. Second, one ruleset should cover every team size, so that learning 1v1 also teaches 3v3. Third, the queue should be physical and social (pads you can see) and backed by an instant global button. Fourth, ranked integrity depends on account friction. Fifth, a draft hook brings players in but does not keep them. OVERKILL's spike and fade, compared with RIVALS' stability, suggests retention comes from feel, ranked goals and a steady content cadence. The draft is what sets the game apart, and the shooter underneath is what keeps players.

## Gauntlet: Glitched supplies the draft grammar, not the blueprint

**Evidence.** Valorant's mode launched with Patch 13.06 on September 22, 2026 ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/valorant-patch-13-06-adds-new-gauntlet-mode-warden-rifle-and-agent-mastery/en)). It puts **16 players into 8 duos** in a tournament that runs until one duo remains ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/valorant-gauntlet-glitched-mode/)). Players control a KAY/O-like "Robo-Agent" and build its kit from other Agents' abilities. Before each round they draft either a new ability or an upgrade from L1 to L3. Reaching L2 means choosing one of two branches, and an L3 upgrade can change what an ability does, not just how strong it is ([valorant-spind.de](https://www.valorant-spind.de/en/valorant-gauntlet-glitched.html); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/gauntlet-glitched-in-valorant-how-the-2v2-roguelike-mode-works/)). Glitches are a "rare, random chance" from L2 onward. Riot publishes no probability. Glitches transform the ability (Healing Orb becomes a one-source shield, for example), and each glitch has its own L3 ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/valorant-patch-13-06-adds-new-gauntlet-mode-warden-rifle-and-agent-mastery/en); [VALORANT Wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Gauntlet:_Glitched)). There is no credit economy. Each player picks one of three weapons per round from tiers that rise by stage ([games.gg](https://games.gg/valorant/guides/valorant-gauntlet-glitched-guide/)). Duos share a health pool. Guide sources report 100 HP with loss damage rising from −10 to −30 across seven stages and 40-second rounds, but this rests on one or two sources ([valorant-spind.de](https://www.valorant-spind.de/en/valorant-gauntlet-glitched.html)). Matches reportedly last 8–15 minutes. Reception evidence is thin: one positive forum thread asking for "upgrades with drawbacks," and a launch bug that trapped players in the Death Cam ([VLR.gg](https://www.vlr.gg/758533/gauntlet-glitch); [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/performance/gauntlet-glitched-death-cam-issue)). Timer length, offer count and pairing logic were not found.

Adjacent games fill in the missing design lessons. Overwatch 2's Stadium found that build-crafting was the most-praised part of its playtests. Blizzard also admitted that "snowballing was also an issue... even with bounties," and reverted a best-of-5 change because players missed "glorious comeback runs" ([Blizzard: Lessons Learned](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24191421/enter-the-stadium-lessons-learned-from-the-playtests/); [Blizzard Director's Take](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24246201)). Lopsided matches and matchmaking imbalance were its top complaints ([Blizzard Forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/so-is-stadium-a-failure/972764)). Rocket League Rumble gives **both teams the same power-up queue in the same order** online ([Rocket League wiki](https://rocketleague.fandom.com/wiki/Rumble)). Dota 2 Ability Draft runs **7 seconds per pick** and assigns a random ability on timeout ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Ability_Draft)). Slay the Spire's hidden rarity offset starts at −5% and rises +1% for each common card shown, as bad-luck protection ([StS wiki](https://slay-the-spire.fandom.com/wiki/Card_Rewards)). Hades gates Duo boons behind owning prerequisite boons ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-what-are-duo-boons-how-get-duo-boons)). Game-design writing distinguishes randomness shown before a decision ("input") from randomness after it ("output"), and argues the first supports strategy ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/the-2-types-of-randomness-and-how-to-apply-them-to-your-board-game)). An academic study warns that input randomness is not automatically satisfying either ([arXiv 2107.08437](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.08437v1.pdf)).

**Recommendation.** Take Valorant's draft grammar: add or upgrade, branch at L2, a function-changing L3, rare glitches from L2 with their own L3, and stakes that rise by stage. Change everything around it. The table below shows where this design deliberately differs from Valorant, so the game does not read as a copy.

| Element | Valorant Gauntlet: Glitched (sourced) | This design (recommendation) |
|---|---|---|
| Where drafting lives | One 16-player LTM | Every queue (1v1–4v4), plus a Gauntlet event |
| Character | KAY/O-like Robo-Agent with borrowed Agent abilities | Original character and original abilities in four readable roles; no borrowed IP |
| Weapons | Pick 1 of 3 per round, tiered by stage | Persistent loadout in standard queues (RIVALS-like, all ranked weapons free); stage-tiered weapon offers only in Gauntlet |
| Glitches | Rare hidden roll, odds unpublished | Shown as an opt-in draft card with stated odds, pity, a visible tell, and some deliberate trade-off cards |
| Catch-up | Rising stakes per stage | Loser gets a better offer (extra card + reroll) in standard queues; shared HP pool only in Gauntlet |
| Team play | Duo shared HP | Teammate "link" synergies (Hades-Duo style) in 2v2+ |
| Fairness | Not documented | Mirrored offer seeds across teams (Rumble style) |

## One ruleset from 1v1 to 4v4, drafted every round

### The pre-match flow moves from lobby to first shot in about a minute

**Evidence.** RIVALS keeps players busy while they wait, with a shooting range, weapon testing and repeated challenges in the lobby, and it starts matches with a map vote (search summary: [GameBoost](https://gameboost.com/blog/roblox-rivals-maps-ranked)). Call of Duty's matchmaking blog reports that long waits lead players to cancel and re-queue, which makes matchmaking worse (search summary: [Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2024/01/call-of-duty-update-an-Inside-look-at-matchmaking)). Third-party FTUE guidance says to deliver something fun within about 10 seconds of joining ([BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-player-retention)).

**Recommendation.** The flow below keeps every waiting moment playable. The accept prompt appears only where a no-show is expensive.

| Step | What happens | Target time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby | Social hub: pads for Duels/Duos/Squads, a Gauntlet board with countdown, aim range, practice dummies that use drafted abilities, spectator screens of live arenas | — | Physical pads plus a menu "Play" button; ticking several queues at once is allowed |
| Queue | Ticket enters local pool first, then the global pool after ~10–20 s | Median <30 s (1v1/2v2) | Show elapsed time, estimated wait and "widening search…"; player stays free to roam |
| Match found | Casual: instant move. Ranked/Gauntlet: full-screen accept, 10 s | 0–10 s | A declined ranked accept triggers a short lockout |
| Transfer | Local arena (casual, if space) or teleport to a reserved match server | 5–15 s | Custom teleport GUI shows the opponent card |
| Opening draft | Confirm loadout (primary + secondary), draft first ability (1 of 3, one card per role) | 20 s | Map vote runs at the same time |
| Rounds | Elimination round → 12 s draft → next round | ~60 s per cycle | Draft screen shows enemy builds live |
| Match end | Results, rewards, 10–15 s rematch vote | 15 s | Rematch reuses the server; otherwise return to lobby with auto-requeue |

### Match and round format per queue

**Evidence.** RIVALS uses first to 5 round wins at every team size ([bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/gamemodes)). OVERKILL uses first to 5 with stacking upgrades, and ROUNDS plays to about 4–5 wins ([profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/overkill); [Landfall: ROUNDS](https://landfall.se/rounds)). Stadium plays first to 3 in Quick Play and first to 4 in Ranked ([Blizzard news](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24226737/)).

**Recommendation.** Keep the first-to-5 elimination structure everywhere so it reads instantly. Vary only team size and arena scale. Players reset to full HP each round and do not respawn within a round. A round lasts 50 seconds; if it is unresolved, an overtime zone shrinks toward the arena centre for 15 seconds, and if it is still unresolved, the side with more total HP remaining wins. A first-to-5 match has at most 9 rounds. With the opening draft that gives up to 9 picks, which exactly fills three abilities at L3. A full build therefore exists only in a deciding round, which makes the late rounds feel climactic.

| Queue | Team size | Win condition | Arena | Expected length | Launch phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Duels | 1v1 | First to 5 rounds | Small | 5–9 min | MVP (casual), Phase 2 (ranked) |
| Duos | 2v2 | First to 5 | Small–medium | 6–10 min | MVP |
| Squads | 3v3, or 4v4 when the pool allows | First to 5 | Medium | 7–11 min | Phase 2 |
| Gauntlet | 8 duos (16) | Last duo with team HP | 4 parallel duo arenas | 10–15 min | Phase 2 (scheduled event) |
| Practice | 1v1 vs labeled bot | First to 3 | Small | 3–5 min | MVP (FTUE + queue filler) |

### The draft: three cards, mirrored seeds, loser advantages, opt-in glitches

**Evidence.** The design tools here are all sourced patterns. Rumble mirrors its randomness across teams ([Rocket League wiki](https://rocketleague.fandom.com/wiki/Rumble)). CS2 escalates its loss bonus by $500 per straight loss up to a cap and resets it on a win ([csdb.gg](https://csdb.gg/guides/economy-guide/)). Stadium shows that bounties did not stop snowballing ([Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24191421/enter-the-stadium-lessons-learned-from-the-playtests)). Vampire Survivors offers Reroll, Skip and Banish ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1794680/discussions/0/3276941718958957262)). Brotato's reroll cost rises within a shop ([Brotato wiki](https://brotato.wiki.spellsandguns.com/Shop)). Riot's TFT team concluded that economy and scaling augments should not appear early ([Riot TFT /Dev](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-dragonlands-learnings/)). Dota denial picks show that seeing the opponent's choices creates counter-play ([bo3.gg](https://bo3.gg/dota2/articles/dota-2-ability-draft-guide)). Stadium players valued "countering your opponents' builds" ([Blizzard Director's Take](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24246201)).

**Recommendation.** Both sides draft after every round, so nobody sits idle, while the round loser gets a structurally better offer. This is a deliberate departure from OVERKILL's loser-only picks. It keeps winners engaged and still gives a real comeback path. A loser-only variant is worth an A/B test in Duels, where ROUNDS proved the model.

| Parameter | Recommended starting value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Ability slots | 3 (matches 2–3 mobile ability buttons) | Readability on touch; 9 total "levels" fill a first-to-5 match |
| Offer size | 3 cards; round loser sees 4 | Pick-1-of-3 is the roguelike standard; extra card = catch-up without currency |
| Card types | New ability (L1), upgrade (L1→L2 branch A/B, L2→L3), glitch, trade-off ("Overclock": stronger effect with a cost, per VLR feedback) | Gauntlet grammar plus requested drawback choices |
| Upgrade weighting | Upgrade-card share rises each round (≈30% → 70%) | Builds converge rather than staying wide and shallow |
| Fairness seed | 1v1: identical offer seed for both players; team modes: slot-for-slot mirrored seeds | Removes most "they got luckier" complaints |
| Rerolls | 1 per match baseline; +1 for each round lost (cap 3) | Escalating, capped loss bonus in CS2's style, without an economy |
| Rarity floor | After 2 straight round losses, guarantee one L3 or synergy card if eligible | Structural catch-up |
| Time gating | Scaling/"snowball" cards cannot appear before round 3 | TFT lesson |
| Synergy ("Link") cards | Appear only when a teammate owns a qualifying ability (2v2+) | Hades Duo gating; rewards team drafting and party play |
| Visibility | Enemy abilities, levels, branches and glitches shown on the draft screen and on nameplates | Enables counter-drafting |
| Timer | 12 s between rounds, 20 s opening; skips when all players lock in | Dota proves sub-10 s picks work; Gauntlet shows little time is needed |
| Auto-pick on timeout | Upgrade the highest-level owned ability; otherwise the first card | Never punishes AFK teammates with junk |

Glitches deserve their own rules because they are the mode's signature and its biggest frustration risk. Each eligible upgrade card (an L2 or L3 card for an owned ability) has a small chance, about 8% to start, of appearing as its glitched alternative. A hidden pity offset in Slay the Spire's style raises that chance by a few points for each draft without a glitch, up to a cap. The odds and the pity rule are shown in a help panel. A glitch is always shown as an optional card naming exactly what the ability becomes, so the player can decline it. Glitches are sidegrades that change an ability's role, not straight nerfs. Each has its own L3 and a distinct colour or shader tell that opponents can read in a fight. In Ranked, glitch rolls come from the shared seed, so both players see the same glitch opportunities. In casual and Gauntlet, rolls can be independent for more variety. If a glitch ever triggers during play, as a live event, it should only help the player, following Into the Breach ([boardgamedesign newsletter](https://boardgamedesign.beehiiv.com/p/019-input-vs-output-randomness-a-couple-of-words-out-of-order-and-more)).

A launch ability set of about 16 abilities across four roles (mobility, damage, control, sustain) gives enough variety for a three-card offer. Each ability has one L1, two L2 branches and two L3s, which is five gameplay states per ability. Roughly six of them should also get glitch variants. That is about 80+ distinct tuned states, and content production at this scale is the main cost driver of the whole project.

### Weapons stay a loadout, so skins carry value and drafts carry decisions

**Evidence.** RIVALS' loadout model supports cosmetic attachment to specific weapons, and the game caps ranked swaps at two per duel ([bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/gamemodes)). Gauntlet's per-round weapon offers remove buy-menu time ([games.gg](https://games.gg/valorant/guides/valorant-gauntlet-glitched-guide/)).

**Recommendation.** In Duels, Duos and Squads, players bring a primary and secondary chosen before the match from a pool in which every ranked-legal weapon is free. They may change weapons twice per match during draft windows. That keeps the per-round decision focused on abilities, keeps players attached to the weapons they skin, and separates the game from Valorant's weapon offers. Stage-tiered weapon offers are reserved for the Gauntlet, where an escalating, roguelike run is the point.

## The Gauntlet becomes a scheduled 16-player event, not a queue you wait in

**Evidence.** Valorant's reported structure is 8 duos with 100 HP each. Each round pits a duo against a different duo, and losses cost −10, −10, −20, −20, −20, −30, −30 across 7 stages of 3 rounds ([valorant-spind.de](https://www.valorant-spind.de/en/valorant-gauntlet-glitched.html); [VALORANT Wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Gauntlet:_Glitched)). The pairing algorithm is not documented. Valorant lets duo partners queue with **no rank restriction**, giving Diamond with Bronze as Riot's example (search summary: [games.gg](https://games.gg/valorant/guides/valorant-gauntlet-glitched-guide/)). One `TeleportAsync` call moves up to 50 players ([TeleportService API](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/TeleportService.yaml)). Roblox's `SocialService` exposes Experience Events APIs, including `PromptRsvpToEventAsync`, for scheduled happenings ([SocialService API](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SocialService.yaml)). No rigorous public study defines a queue-abandon threshold. Anecdotes put quitting at around 5–10 minutes, and a low-authority primer suggests 30–90 seconds for quick modes (search summary: [NeuralBase](https://theneuralbase.com/ai-for-gaming/learn/beginner/matchmaking-quality/)). BedWars' limited "Hyper Kits" mode produced a +12.4% daily bump ([RoWatcher BedWars](https://rowatcher.com/games/2619619496)).

**Recommendation.** Keep 16 players, duos and a shared team health pool, because the format keeps everyone playing several rounds, unlike single elimination, which removes half the lobby after one fight. Retune the format for shorter Roblox sessions and so it is not a copy. Use **5 stages of 3 rounds, with 100 team HP and losses costing 15/15/25/25/35 by stage.** Under this tuning, a duo that loses every round goes 45 → 90 → eliminated in round 7, the hard ceiling is 15 rounds, and with ~60-second cycles a full run lasts 10–15 minutes. All 16 players run in one reserved server with a central draft hub and **four identical duo arenas**. Players move between hub and arenas by in-server CFrame moves, not teleports. Pairing is Swiss-style: avoid repeat opponents and pair duos with similar HP. An odd duo out fights a "ghost" (a bot copy of an eliminated duo's build) or takes a no-damage bye. The last two duos play head-to-head until one reaches zero. Weapons come as stage-tiered pick-1-of-3 offers, and drafting follows the standard rules with independent seeds, since eight teams average out luck.

Queue presentation decides whether the mode survives at low population. Use two entry points. The first is a **persistent Gauntlet ticket** that lets players keep playing Duels. When 16 are ready, a "Gauntlet ready 16/16, accept" prompt appears at the end of their current duel, and a live "11/16 queued" board in the lobby builds anticipation. The second is a **scheduled "Gauntlet Hour"** every 30–60 minutes at peak times, published as a Roblox Experience Event with RSVP and a two-minute check-in window. At T-0, brackets fill with checked-in players. Short brackets fall back to 12 or 8 players, with HP loss scaled up so length stays similar, and any remaining slots get clearly labeled bots, which makes that run unrated. Solo players are paired into duos by MMR proximity. Premade duos have no rank limit (Valorant precedent), but duo MMR still drives pairing. A partner who disconnects is replaced by a bot, and the remaining player keeps full placement rewards. There is never a pause. Rating uses one OpenSkill multi-team update at the end, ranked by elimination order, with ties broken by HP remaining before the final round. Rewards are placement-based cosmetic currency, which makes the Gauntlet the game's recurring "event night" and its strongest creator-content hook.

## A single matchmaker on MemoryStore keeps low-population queues alive

**Evidence.** Roblox's docs explicitly list skill-based matchmaking as a MemoryStore use case ([Roblox docs: Memory stores](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud-services/memory-stores/index.md)). The quotas are **64 KB + 1.2 KB per user of memory** and **1,000 + 120 × CCU request units per minute**, game-wide. `SortedMap:GetRangeAsync` costs one unit per returned item, and values can be up to 32 KB. Sorted maps and queues each live on one partition, which throttles at roughly 30,000 units per minute. The status-code table separately cites 100,000, and the docs do not reconcile the two ([same source](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud-services/memory-stores/index.md); [Partitions](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud-services/memory-stores/per-partition-limits.md)). MemoryStore queues hide read items for an invisibility timeout, and a community thread notes that you cannot remove a player from a queue by key, which complicates cancel flows (search summary: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/player-matchmaking-with-memory-service/1760150)). MessagingService is best-effort, "typically within 1–2 seconds," with a 1 kB message cap ([MessagingService API](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/MessagingService.yaml)).

Several older teleport APIs are now deprecated: `ReserveServer`, `TeleportPartyAsync`, `TeleportToPrivateServer` and client-side `Teleport`. The current path is `ReserveServerAsync` plus server-side `TeleportAsync` with `TeleportOptions`. Teleport data is client-visible and unencrypted. Teleports can fail late through `TeleportInitFailed`, and they do not work in Studio playtests ([TeleportService API](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/TeleportService.yaml); [Roblox docs: Teleport](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/projects/teleport.md)). The Party API (`Player.PartyId`, `SocialService:GetPartyAsync`) returns party members across servers and is meant for matchmaking ([SocialService API](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/SocialService.yaml)). Roblox's Custom Matchmaking, generally available since about November 2025, scores only *public* servers and filters out reserved servers. It can therefore cluster lobbies but cannot form duels ([Roblox docs: Matchmaking](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/matchmaking/index.md)). The best open-source reference, g0ofycat's MIT-licensed "Match" module, uses one sorted map per mode and sub-mode. Its Elo window starts at ±200 and widens by 50 every 5-second loop, it uses 10-second player locks to prevent double-matching, and it retries teleports three times ([GitHub: g0ofycat/Match](https://github.com/g0ofycat/Match)). The older MatchmakingService still cites outdated limits ([GitHub: steven4547466/MatchmakingService](https://github.com/steven4547466/MatchmakingService)).

**Recommendation.** Use two places, a Lobby and a Match place, and set the Match place's access control to "Secure within universe only." Give each queue one sorted map (`Q_Duels`, `Q_Duos`, `Q_Squads`, `Q_Gauntlet`, plus ranked variants). The key is the ticket ID (party or user), the sort key is MMR, and the value is a compact ~250-byte record {members, mmr, input class, region, enqueuedAt, jobId} with a ~120-second TTL refreshed by heartbeat. Sorted maps allow cancel-by-key and MMR-window range reads. A hash map holds per-player state (InQueue / Matched / InMatch), match manifests (teams, draft seed, access code) and a **leader lock** that elects one lobby server as matchmaker. The cost math makes this almost mandatory. At 50 CCU the game-wide budget is 7,000 units per minute. One matchmaker scanning 5 queues of ~20 tickets every 2 seconds uses about 3,000. Five lobby servers each doing the same would use about 15,000.

The handoff works like this. The matchmaker writes the manifest and calls `ReserveServerAsync` once. Each lobby server notices its players' state change by polling, with an optional MessagingService hint, and teleports them with the access code. The match server reads the manifest from MemoryStore and trusts nothing in teleport data beyond a match ID. Wrap every call in pcall with backoff, and handle `TeleportInitFailed` by re-queuing players at high priority.

Casual play uses a RIVALS-style hybrid. If enough compatible tickets exist in the same lobby, start a local arena immediately. After 10–20 seconds, the ticket moves to the global pool and a reserved server. Ranked and Gauntlet always use reserved servers, for clean state and leaver detection. Size lobbies at 30–40 players with 6–8 arena instances. The draft runs on the match server, where it is authoritative.

Parties use the native Party API first, with an in-game invite fallback that writes to the target player's hash-map inbox. A party is one ticket. Its MMR leans toward the top player (for example 0.6 × max + 0.4 × mean) to deter alt boosting, and ranked parties must fall within a spread cap (RIVALS uses 800 Elo). Avoid backfill in 1v1 and Ranked. Casual Squads may backfill only in the first round. A rematch vote reuses the same reserved server with a fresh draft. Leaving Ranked gives an automatic loss and an escalating lockout (5, then 15, then 60 minutes within 24 hours). Casual leavers get 1–2 minute lockouts if they repeat.

Low population is the real launch threat. Ship as few queues as possible: Duels and Duos, with Squads folded into one flexible queue that targets 4v4 and drops to 3v3 after 45 seconds. Let players tick several queues at once, with removal from all of them on match via the state lock. Show cached per-queue counts that the matchmaker writes every 5–10 seconds. At very low numbers, show "players online" and an estimated wait rather than "1 in queue." Do not split by region at launch. Prefer same-continent tickets only for the first ~20 seconds. Targets are a 1v1/2v2 median under 30 seconds with p90 under 90 seconds, Squads p90 under 2 minutes, and Gauntlet at 2–5 minutes only while players are playing something else. Bots are allowed in tutorials, practice-while-queued and emergency Gauntlet fill. They are always labeled, never appear in Ranked, and never change ratings.

## Ranked launches as 1v1 only, with gates and a hidden OpenSkill rating

**Evidence.** OpenSkill (Weng-Lin) supports multi-team and asymmetric matches, is peer-reviewed in JOSS, claims TrueSkill-level accuracy, and offers an ordinal for leaderboards and an Elo-style display mapping ([GitHub: openskill.py](https://github.com/vivekjoshy/openskill.py)). A Luau port ships inside MatchmakingService ([GitHub](https://github.com/steven4547466/MatchmakingService)). In May 2026, Apex Legends lengthened its strict skill window from 30 to 60 seconds before widening and saw no significant rise in queue time, at Apex's scale (search summary: [EA](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/overclocked-matchmaking-update)). A vendor source warns that tight skill bands empty small pools (search summary: [Edgegap](https://edgegap.com/glossary/sbmm)). RIVALS shows 200-Elo tiers, 10 placements capped at Diamond I, decay at the top tier only, a daily loss shield at lower ranks, and a separate unbuyable "Glory" currency for ranked cosmetics ([RIVALS wiki: Ranked](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranked); [RIVALS Wiki: Currencies](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)). Thresholds differ between guides.

**Recommendation.** Launch Ranked Duels only. It is the largest pool and the cleanest to rate, and team ranked can follow once Duos sustains roughly 10+ concurrent queuers. Store a hidden {μ, σ} per queue. Seed a new queue's rating from the player's best other queue with σ raised. Show a visible points ladder of 200 points per tier that converges on hidden MMR, with 10 placements capped at a mid-high tier. The search window starts at ±100 display points, holds for 15–30 seconds (a server-tunable value), widens by 50 every 5 seconds, and accepts any opponent after 90 seconds. Pair high-σ newcomers with each other first. Gate Ranked behind account age (≥14 days), a level threshold and roughly 10 casual wins. Reserve ID or age verification for top-tier leaderboards and prize-bearing Gauntlet events, which also matches Roblox's 2026 age-check direction.

All abilities, branches and glitches must be available in Ranked from day one, because in a draft game the draft *is* the skill expression. Brawl Stars shows how persistent unlocks split a player base into "maxed" and "sub-competitive" ([GuildOrder](https://guildorder.com/th/games/brawl_stars/guides/brawler-mastery-progression)). Seasons reset visible rank and award a Glory-like currency for prestige cosmetics that cannot be bought.

## Server-validated hitscan, Server Authority movement and touch-first controls

### Shots are validated against a rewound hitbox

**Evidence.** Roblox's official Laser Tag and FPS templates use client camera raycasts sent to the server. The server re-derives spread from shared code and checks the shot angle, the target's proximity to the claimed hit point, and line of sight via a server raycast. Fire rate is owned by the server, and damage lives server-side as an attribute ([Roblox Docs: Detect hits](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/gameplay-scripting/detect-hits); [Implement blasters](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/gameplay-scripting/implement-blasters)). Roblox's security docs say the client should send origin and claimed hit, "not who to damage" ([Client-server boundary](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/client-server-boundary)). Server Authority does **not** cover hit detection. Remote events are reportedly 40–50 ms out of sync with simulation, and Roblox calls shooting mispredictions normal while it explores time-synced events (search summary: [DevForum engineer insights](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/server-authority-studio-beta-insights-from-engineers/4139321); [Server authority model](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/server-authority)). The community RollbackHitbox library ring-buffers hitboxes every Heartbeat and clamps rewind. Its author says it does not stop aimbots (search summary: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rollbackhitbox-server-authoritative-lag-compensation-for-roblox-shooters-open-source/4553295)). Two earlier rewind libraries, Rewind and SecureCast, are archived. A skeptical thread argues that lag compensation on Roblox is inaccurate, citing engine limits (search summary: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/lag-compensation-is-inaccurate/1419968)). The available DevForum opinion rates popular gun kits as ill-suited to serious games. ACS reportedly had a public "kill all" exploit, and Carbon Engine is obfuscated (search summary, mostly 2023: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-are-the-best-weapon-systems-preferably-fe-for-a-game-with-a-lot-of-shooting/2716544)). No public technical documentation exists for RIVALS', Phantom Forces' or Bad Business' netcode. Cheat vendors advertising "silent aim" suggest those games accept client hit claims to some degree ([GitHub: Phantom Forces cheat](https://github.com/Herrtt/Phantom-Forces-Cheat)).

**Recommendation.** Build a custom framework forked from Roblox's unobfuscated Laser Tag/FPS template rather than adopting ACS, Carbon or FE Gun Kit. Most guns are hitscan. The client sends {shotId, `GetServerTimeNow` timestamp, origin, direction, claimed part and position}. The server checks for finite numbers, its own ammo and cooldown, origin distance, the angle against seeded spread, line of sight, and finally the claimed hit point against the target's **rewound, inflated hitbox**, with rewind clamped to about 200–250 ms. Small arenas with eight or fewer players make this cheap to compute. Use projectiles only where travel time is part of the gameplay (rockets, grenades, ability projectiles). Design the shot transport so it can be swapped when Roblox ships time-synced events.

Game feel is all client-side, from the first frame:

- fire on input *begin*, which Roblox's tutorial shows feels better than firing on release
- spring-based viewmodel recoil and camera kick, kept separate from a visual shake the player cannot counter
- pooled tracers and sounds
- an optimistic hit marker, upgraded to a confirmed marker and damage number on the server's acknowledgement
- a kill feed driven only by the server

### Anti-cheat assumes the client is compromised

**Evidence.** Roblox's rewritten security docs list what exploiters can do. They can fire any remote at any rate with arbitrary arguments, take ownership of their own physics, and send NaN values that pass `typeof` checks. The docs recommend token buckets, suppression first and a suspicion score instead of instant bans, honeypot remotes, and the Ban API for persistent offenders ([Security tactics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/security-tactics); [Server-side detection](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/server-side-detection)). Server Authority reached full release in mid-2026. It moves movement and physics to the server with client prediction and rollback, blocking speed, fly and teleport exploits. It also requires several settings: Input Action System player scripts, deferred signals, fixed simulation, StreamingEnabled, and a 64-attribute replication limit ([Server authority model](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/server-authority); search summary: [DevForum: Full Release](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-ship-fair-and-competitive-games-with-server-authority/4727993)). Roblox's network-ownership page still calls it "beta," which conflicts with the release posts ([Network ownership](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/network-ownership)). No official 2026 statement on Hyperion's status or platform coverage was found. Its coverage on mobile, Mac and console is unconfirmed ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/exploit-prevention-update/2663101?page=68)).

**Recommendation.** Turn on Server Authority from the first prototype. Retrofitting it later is far harder, because it dictates input, signal and streaming architecture. Map each exploit to a server-side counter:

- **Rapid fire:** token buckets.
- **Wall shots:** line-of-sight raycasts.
- **Hitbox expanders:** rewound-hitbox checks.
- **Silent aim:** compare each shot against a low-rate camera look vector sent on an UnreliableRemoteEvent.
- **Aimbot:** statistical heuristics (headshot rate, snap velocity, cadence) feeding a suspicion score that escalates from quiet mitigation to matchmaking restriction and then manual review.
- **ESP:** never replicate hidden state, such as an invisible enemy's position, to opposing clients.

Do not count on Hyperion, since most players are on mobile. Every drafted ability is extra attack surface and needs the same validation as guns.

### Abilities predict on the client and resolve on the server

**Evidence.** DevForum consensus is that the server owns cooldowns and effects, and the client timer is "only a UI display" (search summary: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/should-abilities-be-managed-on-the-server-or-the-client/3073053)). Roblox's Character Controller Library, in beta, defines abilities as deterministic Luau that runs in both the predicted client and the authoritative server simulation, with timed cooldown labels and action slots that appear as touch buttons ([CCL custom abilities](https://create.roblox.com/docs/characters/character-controller-library/custom-abilities)). Server Authority's techniques doc recommends attribute-driven VFX state machines, undoing mispredicted effects, and predictive instance creation for projectiles ([Server authority techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/server-authority/techniques)).

**Recommendation.** Use a shared `AbilityDefinitions` module holding data and pure functions for every ability, level, branch and glitch. The server holds each player's drafted loadout, locked at the end of each draft, plus `cooldownEndsAt` timestamps and charges. On cast, the client plays effects instantly and sends {abilityId, castId, aim, time}. The server validates loadout, cooldown (with about 100 ms of grace), state, range and line of sight, then broadcasts the cast so other clients render VFX from pooled assets. Movement abilities (dash, blink, double jump) go through CCL or BindToSimulation so they predict smoothly. Because CCL is still in beta, keep a Humanoid-based fallback.

### Mobile is the primary platform, so design for touch first

**Evidence.** The Input Action System is fully released. Unlike `ContextActionService`, it does not auto-generate touch buttons, and a reported bug with input-ended on mobile UI buttons affects hold-to-fire ([Input Action System](https://create.roblox.com/docs/input/input-action-system); [simplified.media](https://simplified.media/guides/roblox-input-actions); [DevForum bug](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/issue-with-inputaction-system-not-detecting-input-ended-using-a-ui-button-on-mobile-devices/3897882)). Roblox's official Aim Assist module turns on automatically for gamepad and touch in its FPS templates (search summary: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/get-aim-assist-in-studio-templates-or-add-it-to-your-experience/4082644)). A DevForum developer warns that aim assist "makes it unfair to PC players, especially if your game is movement based" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/help-with-aim-assist/3802674)). Roblox's Device Type matchmaking signal affects only public-server placement ([Attributes and signals](https://create.roblox.com/docs/matchmaking/attributes-and-signals)).

**Recommendation.** The mobile layout has a dynamic left thumbstick and a right-side camera drag area. On the right are a large fire button, an optional second fire button for claw grips, ADS, reload, jump, and **three drafted-ability buttons in an arc** whose icons show level pips and glitch tint. Every element can be moved and resized, saved per player. Use the official Aim Assist module on touch and gamepad only, never on mouse, at modest strength. Offer optional auto-fire-on-target limited to the weapon's fire rate with a short dwell. Never give stat buffs by device, because device detection can be spoofed. Record the player's preferred input at queue time. In Ranked, run a KBM pool and a touch-plus-gamepad pool, with opt-in cross-input. Casual queues merge inputs freely to protect pool size. The draft UI needs large, icon-led cards with one-line descriptions that a 13-year-old can read on a phone in 12 seconds.

## Cosmetics pay, drafts stay free, and Roblox policy shapes the shop

**Evidence.** Top shooters monetize almost entirely through cosmetics. RIVALS sells wraps, skins, charms and finishers using Keys, a currency players can earn or buy. It runs a 5-item Daily Shop and a 70-tier free/premium season pass whose XP comes from round wins *or* playtime, and it keeps a separate Glory currency for ranked rewards ([Rivals Fandom: Keys](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Keys); [Rivals Miraheze: Shop](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Shop); [RIVALS Wiki: Season Pass](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Season_Pass)). Since June 2026 it also grants free keys and weapon trials through ads ([Rivals Fandom: Updates](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Updates)). A third-party guide says one Season 3 weapon was on the premium track only. That would break a pure cosmetics model and is unverified ([dungeonpath](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/rivals/season-3-battle-pass-guide/)). RIVALS revenue estimates range from about $1.7M to $15M+ per month across trackers, which is useful only as a ceiling ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/6035872082/rivals); [RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)). Arsenal grants +25% currency and XP for joining its group ([Roblox: Arsenal](https://www.roblox.com/games/286090429/Arsenal)).

Policy tightened through 2026. Roblox treats a paid random item as anything random bought with Robux, *including via an intermediate currency bought with Robux* ([Creator Docs: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). These items are restricted for UK and Australian minors, and in Brazil for minors from March 17, 2026 and for unverified adults from March 31, 2026 ([DevForum UK](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-paid-random-items-restriction-for-uk-users-under-18/3072183); [Bloxy News](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/2034153291899408514)). A May 2026 expansion reportedly covers pity systems and luck boosts, with stricter odds disclosure ([RoUtility News](https://x.com/RoUtilityNews/status/2059344970994053348)). Chat now requires an age check globally, and chat is limited to adjacent age bands ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat)). Maturity labels determine which audience can find the game. Moderate allows "light realistic blood," and the newer Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15) tiering appears to be 2026 and should be confirmed in the live docs ([Creator Docs: Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)). Creator Rewards replaced Premium Payouts on July 24, 2025 ([Roblox 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051082/rblx-20260630.htm)). DevEx pays about $0.0038 per Robux, and from June 8, 2026 a 42%-higher rate applies to spend by age-checked US adults in eligible R15 games ([DevForum: US 18+ DevEx](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091)). New items are enrolled in Managed Pricing by default ([Creator Docs: Managed pricing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing)).

**Recommendation.** The rule is that **nothing that changes a draft or a gunfight is sold.** Sell ability VFX skins, including "glitch-themed" alternate effects that are visually distinct but still readable to opponents, as well as weapon wraps, finishers, charms, emotes and lobby banners. Sell them by direct purchase and through a rotating daily shop, with no Robux-purchasable boxes at all. That avoids the paid-random-items compliance work across three regions and protects against further restrictions. Run a free and premium season pass with XP from round wins or playtime, so losing players still progress. Keep any new gameplay content on the free track. Add a Glory-style ranked and Gauntlet currency that cannot be bought, ability "mastery contracts" that reward cosmetics for using each ability, rewarded-ad currency where available, and an Arsenal-style group-join bonus.

New players start with a curated pool of about 8 abilities so their first drafts are readable. The full pool unlocks within the first several matches through play, never through purchase, and Ranked always uses the full pool. Use stylized, bloodless hit effects so the game can target a Mild maturity label and the widest discoverable audience. Support R15 for the adult DevEx rate. Build pings, a quick-chat wheel and emotes as the main communication tools, because many players will not have chat.

## Discovery now rewards retention and co-play over clicks

**Evidence.** Roblox's Recommended For You ranks games on per-user averages, which does not penalize small games with engaged players ([DevForum: Recommended For You](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)). Secondary sources report a June 15, 2026 update that widened the retention window from 7 to 28 days. They describe first-play bounce rate as a top negative signal and say intentional co-play days and spend days now carry more weight. The sources partly conflict on whether qPTR was removed ([zehn-studio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/); [gmmarket](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/how-the-roblox-discovery-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026-myths-debunked)). The GameAnalytics 2026 benchmark covers 500+ games with 1M+ MAU. Its medians are **D1 10.3%, D7 1.6% and D30 0.5%**, and the top 1% reach D1 22.2%, D7 9.1% and D30 4.7% ([GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox Report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)). BLOXG's much higher averages come from a sample of already-promoted games ([BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-retention-benchmarks)). Roblox's Friend Referral System rewards both inviter and invitee, can reach non-friends, and attributes joins through `ReferredByPlayerId` ([Creator Docs: Friend referral system](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/referral-system)). Roblox claims sponsored-play costs can fall below $0.01. Third-party figures conflict ([DevForum: Ads RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106); [BLOXG ad benchmarks](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-advertising-benchmarks)). DevForum FTUE advice is to strip pop-ups, hide non-essential UI and instrument funnel events. One developer saw only about 50% finish a tutorial ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/need-feedback-mainly-core-loop-ftue-and-retention/4773215)).

**Recommendation.** Each signal maps to a feature. Bounce rate depends on time-to-first-shot. A new player should be in a 1v1 against a labeled bot within 10–20 seconds, with the first match's draft auto-picked or limited to two obvious cards. Full drafting comes in matches 2–3, and real queues follow. 28-day retention depends on ranked seasons, the pass, weekly Gauntlet nights and a cadence of new abilities and glitches; each update adding new draftable content is the game's natural content hook. Co-play days depend on first-class parties for Duos and Squads, a referral invite button with cosmetic rewards, and Link synergy cards that reward drafting with friends. Spend days depend on the daily shop.

Creator culture is the other half of discovery. Glitch reveals, L3 transformations and late-round full builds should be readable as clips. Spectator cams for eliminated Gauntlet players, private servers for creator tournaments and a "1v1 the devs" event help as well. Hold back paid acquisition until the onboarding flow beats median D1, because buying low-intent players drags down per-user averages. Realistic targets: **D1 at or above 12–16% and D7 at or above 3–5%** would put the game comfortably above median.

## Eight risks that could sink the project

The risks below combine sourced precedents with design judgment. They are listed roughly from most to least likely to cause failure.

| Risk | Evidence or precedent | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Novelty spike, then fade | OVERKILL ~7K → ~500 CCU ([profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/overkill)) | Gunfeel first; ranked, pass and weekly Gauntlet as retention; regular new-ability drops |
| Queues starve at low CCU | MemoryStore quotas scale with CCU; tight bands empty pools ([Roblox docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud-services/memory-stores/index.md); [Edgegap](https://edgegap.com/glossary/sbmm)) | Two launch queues, multi-queue, flexible Squads, scheduled Gauntlet, labeled bots, play while queued |
| Broken builds and snowballing | Stadium's one-shot Cassidy build and snowball admission ([Blizzard](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24191421/enter-the-stadium-lessons-learned-from-the-playtests/)) | Loser-favoring offers, time-gated scaling cards, visible builds, telemetry on pick and win rates, fast hotfix pipeline |
| Cheating erodes ranked | Openly sold RIVALS scripts ([itch.io](https://itch.io/blog/1363626/rivals-script-keyless)) | Server Authority, rewound validation, suspicion scoring, ranked account gates |
| Mobile/PC fairness backlash | Spoofable device flags; aim-assist complaints ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/should-i-give-mobileconsole-players-a-advantage/2051468)) | Feature-based helpers, modest aim assist, input-split ranked pools |
| Discovery shift cuts inflow | Bad Business' 2023 algorithm-driven decline ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-algorithm-changes-are-killing-our-game/2417236)) | Optimize retention and co-play signals; referral and group channels; creator relationships |
| Content scope explodes | ~80+ tuned ability states at launch (design estimate) | 16-ability MVP with glitches on ~6; data-driven ability definitions; cut L3 branches before cutting quality |
| Seen as a Valorant clone, or a policy breach | Valorant mode is recent and visible; PRI rules in UK/AU/BR ([Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)) | Original fiction and abilities, all-queue drafting, persistent loadouts; no paid boxes |

Two technical dependencies also carry schedule risk. The Character Controller Library is still in beta. Server Authority's beta-era known issues, including the missing rollback event for custom state, may or may not be fixed in the full release ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-bindtosimulation-rolling-out-usefixedsimulation/4605516)). Both should be proven in the prototype phase rather than assumed.

## MVP ships Duels and Duos; ranked and Gauntlet follow on metric gates

**Recommendation.** The phases below sequence scope so that each risky system is validated before the next depends on it. The gates are design targets derived from the benchmarks and queue-time guidance above, not sourced industry standards.

| Phase | Scope | Exit gate to next phase |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Prototype (internal) | Server Authority movement; hitscan pipeline with rewind validation; 2 weapons; 6 abilities with L1–L3 branches and 2 glitches; local 1v1 pad arena; draft UI on phone | Playtesters prefer the drafted 1v1 over a no-ability control; draft completes in ≤12 s on mobile; optimistic-hit rejection rate is low |
| 1. MVP / soft launch | Lobby with pads, aim range, practice-bot duels; casual Duels and Duos; local-first plus global MemoryStore matchmaking with a single matchmaker; Party API parties; ~16 abilities (~6 with glitches); 6–8 weapons; 3–4 arenas; bot FTUE; direct-buy cosmetics and daily shop; analytics funnel | D1 ≥12%; Duels median queue <30 s at real CCU; no exploit class unpatched for more than a week |
| 2. Competitive layer | Ranked Duels (OpenSkill, gates, placements, input pools); flexible Squads (3v3/4v4); Gauntlet as scheduled Experience Events with persistent tickets and bot fill; season pass with Glory currency; referral rewards | D7 ≥3–5%; Gauntlet fills 16 at scheduled times; ranked queue p90 <90 s |
| 3. Scale and depth | Ranked Duos/Squads; always-on Gauntlet queue; Link synergies expanded; new abilities and glitches each season; private servers for creator tournaments; Gauntlet Solo or Trios variants as rotating events | Sustained CCU supports dedicated queues (~10+ concurrent queuers per mode) |

The cut line for the MVP is clear. Ranked, the Gauntlet and Squads all depend on a population that does not exist on day one. Shipping them early would fragment the pool and raise bounce rate, which is the signal the 2026 algorithm reportedly penalizes most. Gunfeel, the draft loop, onboarding and two fast casual queues are the parts that must be excellent at launch.

## Conclusion

The research points to a single strategic choice. Copying Valorant's 16-player gauntlet as the main mode would make the game depend on its hardest queue to fill. Putting drafting inside cheap, fast 1v1 and 2v2 queues makes every short session a run in itself. Each round becomes a small roguelike decision, and the Gauntlet becomes a draw rather than a requirement. That framing also turns several apparent constraints into design tools. A single elected matchmaker suits MemoryStore's CCU-scaled quotas. Mirrored offer seeds and loser-favoring draft cards provide catch-up without an economy. Showing glitches openly as optional draft cards keeps the randomness fun for players who win or lose by skill.

The bigger open question is whether ability drafting can do for retention what it clearly does for novelty. No Roblox draft shooter has shown that yet, and the only public data point (OVERKILL) argues caution. The project should therefore treat Phase 0 as a real experiment, comparing drafted duels against a plain-gunplay control, and let measured retention, not the appeal of the Gauntlet concept, decide how much of the roadmap gets built.
