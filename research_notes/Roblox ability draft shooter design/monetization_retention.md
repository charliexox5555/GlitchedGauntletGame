# Monetization, Progression, Retention & Discovery for Roblox Competitive Shooters (as of Oct 2026)

> Method note: The research proxy blocked direct fetches of roblox.com domains (create.roblox.com, devforum.roblox.com) and several third-party sites, so findings on official Roblox pages come from search-engine extracts of those pages, not full-page reads. Where a claim comes only from third-party blogs (RoWatcher, BLOXG, rolearn.dev, Loottally, dungeonpath, etc.), it is marked as such. Revenue figures for Roblox games are always third-party models; Roblox and developers do not publish per-game revenue.

## 1. How RIVALS, Arsenal, Bad Business, Phantom Forces monetize (and revenue estimates)

### Takeaway
The top Roblox shooters monetize almost entirely through cosmetics (wraps/skins, finishers, charms, emotes) sold through a soft/premium hybrid currency (RIVALS "Keys", Arsenal "BattleBucks") plus a seasonal premium pass. Weapons and power are earnable by play, and ranked play has its own separate currency. RIVALS is now the genre leader and one of Roblox's top earners, but per-game revenue estimates vary by up to 5x between trackers.

### Cited Findings
**RIVALS (the closest comparable: a 1v1–5v5 duel shooter)**
- Keys are the main currency. They buy Wrap Boxes, Charm Capsules, Finisher Packs and Wraps in the Shop, plus Daily Shop items — [RIVALS Wiki: Keys](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Keys); [RIVALS Wiki: Currencies](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)
- Keys are also earned through play: contracts, tasks, codes, daily login rewards and a Prize Wheel. Robux key bundles are the main paid route, and keys can be gifted — [RIVALS Wiki: Currencies](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies); [rivalsroblox.wiki key gifting guide](https://rivalsroblox.wiki/guide/key-gifting/)
- Contracts reward wraps and finishers for in-game objectives, such as eliminations or damage with a specific weapon. This is effectively weapon-mastery progression delivered as cosmetics — [RIVALS Wiki: Contracts](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Contracts)
- "Glory" is a separate Ranked currency for Ranked Shop cosmetics ("Glorious" wraps, finishers and skins), so ranked prestige cannot be bought — [RIVALS Wiki: Currencies](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)
- The Season Pass has 70 tiers: 69 rewards on the free track and 70 on the "Prime" track. Pass XP comes from winning duel rounds or from playtime ("win 25 rounds or play for 20 minutes" per step, per the wiki) — [RIVALS Wiki: Season Pass](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Season_Pass); [Miraheze RIVALS wiki](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Season_Pass_&_Prime_Season_Pass)
- Season 3 guide (third-party): the free track includes a new weapon (Spear) earnable only through pass XP. The premium track adds another (Grappler), and the premium pass costs 799 Robux. Each season resets rank and the pass and adds new weapons, and ranked rewards are said never to be re-released. Note: putting a weapon on the premium track goes against a pure "cosmetic-only" claim, so verify whether the Grappler can also be earned elsewhere — [dungeonpath S3 guide](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/rivals/season-3-battle-pass-guide/); [dungeonpath season guide](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/rivals/complete-season-guide/)
- Ranked season rewards (third-party guide): a weapon skin after 200 ranked wins, an exclusive katana for top "Archnemesis" finishers, and a charm showing final rank. Sources conflict on pass size: one claims a "Season 1" in Sept 2026 with 100+ tiers, which contradicts the wiki's 70 tiers — [rosenberryrooms](https://www.rosenberryrooms.com/rivals-roblox-season-1-update/); [RIVALS Wiki: Ranked Seasons](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked_Seasons)
- Revenue estimates (all modeled, and inconsistent):
  - RoWatcher game page: about $1.74M/month (confidence 0.78) — [RoWatcher RIVALS](https://rowatcher.com/games/6035872082/rivals)
  - Profitable.app: about $134.3M lifetime and about $4.3M in the last 30 days (Jul 2026), from a modeled formula — [Profitable](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/rivals)
  - RoWatcher news: "$8–15M+/month" from weapon skins and battle passes, and says RIVALS topped PC/mobile revenue rankings in Jan 2026 — [RoWatcher top earners 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform). Note that RoWatcher's own page and its news piece disagree by roughly 5–8x.
- Community criticism exists ("awesome shooter or just a cash grab"), centered on how much the key/box economy dominates — [Medium op-ed](https://medium.com/@serejmesrey/rivals-on-roblox-an-awesome-shooter-or-just-a-cash-grab-fde4417eecec)

**Arsenal (ROLVe)**
- Free-to-play with cosmetic-only purchases: weapon skins, character skins, kill effects, emotes and calling cards. The shop runs on "BattleBucks", with a Daily Shop and Character cases — [ROLVe wiki: Arsenal](https://rolve.fandom.com/wiki/Arsenal); [Roblox wiki: ROLVe/Arsenal](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/ROLVe/Arsenal)
- Group-join incentive: joining the ROLVe group grants a chat tag plus 25% more BattleBucks and XP. This is a cheap community-growth lever — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/286090429/Arsenal)
- Arsenal was reportedly the most-visited Roblox FPS from May 2020 (when it passed Phantom Forces) until May 2025, when RIVALS passed it — [NamuWiki: Arsenal](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox)); [Wikipedia: List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)

**Phantom Forces / Bad Business**
- Phantom Forces is positioned as a more simulation-style shooter. Public trackers show visits and CCU but no revenue — [RoMonitor Phantom Forces](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/292439477/); [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox))

### Inferences
- The proven pattern for a non-P2W Roblox shooter has five parts:
  1. A premium-ish currency that can be both bought and earned.
  2. Cosmetic boxes and direct-buy cosmetics.
  3. A rotating Daily Shop, which creates urgency.
  4. A seasonal free plus premium pass.
  5. A separate, unbuyable ranked currency for prestige cosmetics.

  This maps directly onto an ability-draft shooter: keep abilities earnable or free, and sell ability VFX skins, finishers, wraps and charms.
- If any box or wheel is purchasable with Robux, or with a currency bought with Robux, it falls under Roblox's paid-random-items rules (see Section 2). That includes RIVALS-style Wrap Boxes bought with Keys.
- Treat the RIVALS figures only as an order-of-magnitude ceiling (low single-digit to low double-digit $M/month at peak). Do not use them for business-case planning.

### Gaps
- No reliable source found for Bad Business or Phantom Forces monetization specifics (credits, cases, game passes) or revenue in 2026. Prior (unverified, training-era) knowledge suggests Phantom Forces sells credits and skin cases and Bad Business sells skin cases and a battle pass. Verify in-game.
- No data found on RIVALS private servers, Arsenal battle pass status in 2026, or drop odds in RIVALS or Arsenal boxes.
- No developer-disclosed revenue for any of these four games.

## 2. Roblox policies: paid random items, maturity labels, age checks, payouts, DevEx, pricing

### Takeaway
2026 tightened several rules:
- Paid random items are banned for UK/Australian minors (since 2024) and for all Brazilian users (minors from Mar 17, 2026, non-age-verified adults from Mar 31, 2026), with disclosure rules expanded in May 2026.
- Chat requires an age check globally since Jan 2026.
- Premium Payouts were replaced by Creator Rewards (Jul 24, 2025).
- The standard DevEx rate is about $0.0038/Robux, plus a 42%-higher rate for spend by age-checked 18+ US players (from Jun 8, 2026).
- Managed Pricing unifies regional pricing and price optimization.

### Cited Findings
**Paid random items (loot boxes)**
- Scope (per Creator docs extract): paying Robux to spin a prize wheel counts as a paid random item, and so does buying gems or tickets that are later spent on a wheel. Skill-based gameplay variance is excluded — [Creator Docs: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items); [DevForum: What is considered PRI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-is-considered-paid-random-items/4413243)
- Restricted users are detected via PolicyService `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted`. For them, developers must pick a compliant treatment, e.g. an unpaid/earnable path to the item, or outcomes in a fixed, disclosed order — [Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items); [DevForum: Handling PRI for restricted users](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/handling-paid-random-items-for-users-with-policy-restrictions/4807642)
- Regional timeline:
  - UK under-18 restriction began Aug 2024 — [DevForum UK](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-paid-random-items-restriction-for-uk-users-under-18/3072183)
  - Australia added Sept 2024 — [DevForum Australia](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-paid-random-items-restriction-for-australian-users/3153602)
  - Brazil: under-18s from Mar 17, 2026, and adults who are not age-verified from Mar 31, 2026. Non-compliant experiences "may be subject to moderation" — [Bloxy News (X)](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/2034153291899408514)
- May 26, 2026: the policy expanded to cover paid eggs, crates, spins, pity systems and luck boosts, with stricter odds disclosure and regional compliance via PolicyService — [RoUtility News (X)](https://x.com/RoUtilityNews/status/2059344970994053348); [DevForum: Clarifying Requirements for PRI](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622?page=2)
- Developer guidance: hiding the purchase UI is not enough; enforce server-side with receipt checks. One forum user claims enforcement is lax, but that is anecdotal, not official — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/handling-paid-random-items-for-users-with-policy-restrictions/4807642)

**Content maturity labels**
- There are four labels: Minimal, Mild, Moderate and Restricted. Moderate allows "moderate violence, light realistic blood, moderate crude humor, and/or unplayable gambling content". Restricted allows strong violence, heavy realistic blood, alcohol, strong language and more — [Creator Docs: Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity); [Roblox wiki: Content maturity](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Content_maturity)
- Per the Creator Docs extract:
  - Minimal/Mild games are eligible for "Roblox Kids" (ages 5–8) and "Roblox Select" (ages 9–15).
  - Moderate games reach Roblox Select (9–15) and standard Roblox (16+).
  - Restricted is 18+ age-verified only.
  - The maturity questionnaire is mandatory before publishing.

  This Kids/Select tiering appears new (2026) and should be confirmed in the live docs. One Creator Hub page reportedly mentions a "Content May Vary" label limited to age-checked users 16+, which conflicts with older pages — [Creator Docs: Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity); [rating-system wiki](https://rating-system.fandom.com/wiki/Roblox_content_maturity_labels)

**Age checks (2025–2026)**
- Age checks for chat became mandatory. They started in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands in the first week of Dec 2025, and went global in early Jan 2026. Facial age estimation via Persona is the default, with government ID as an option for users 13+ — [Roblox Newsroom Nov 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat); [Biometric Update Jan 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/roblox-rolls-out-facial-age-estimation-for-chat-access-globally)
- Six age bands: under 9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20 and 21+. Users can chat only within their own band or adjacent bands. Gameplay without chat remains available to users who have not done an age check — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/roblox-now-requires-age-verification-to-use-in-game-chat-163213512.html)
- Accuracy concerns: an average error of about 1.4 years is reported, and the estimates are weaker at the edges of age ranges — [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/roblox-rolls-out-facial-age-estimation-for-chat-access-globally)

**Premium Payouts → Creator Rewards**
- Engagement-Based (Premium) Payouts were replaced by Creator Rewards starting Jul 24, 2025 (SEC filings and Creator docs) — [Roblox 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051082/rblx-20260630.htm); [Creator Docs: Engagement-based payouts](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts)
- Third-party (unverified) description:
  - 5 Robux per "Active Spender" per day when your experience is among their first three that day and they play 10 or more minutes. An Active Spender has spent $9.99 or more in the past 60 days.
  - A "35% revenue share on new players" — not found in Roblox docs.

  [Loottally](https://loottally.com/premium-payouts-calculator); [rolearn.dev guide](https://rolearn.dev/insights/roblox-creator-rewards-payout-guide/)
- Developers report a roughly 60-day hold before engagement payouts reach balances — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/shorten-the-hold-period-of-engagement-payouts-or-restore-the-old-premium-payouts-system/3956591)
- OUTDATED FLAG: any guide that still describes "Premium Payouts" as active (paid on Premium subscribers' share of playtime) is pre-July-2025.

**DevEx**
- Standard rate is about $0.0038/Robux (since Sept 5, 2025; previously $0.0035). Guides that still quote $0.0035 in 2026 are outdated — [rolearn.dev DevEx 2026](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/roblox-devex-requirements-2026/); [gemlist](https://www.gemlist.io/blog/does-roblox-devex-actually-pay)
- US 18+ rate, effective Jun 8, 2026: eligible in-game spend from age-checked 18+ US players cashes out at $0.54 per 100 earned Robux, 42% above standard. The announcement ties eligibility to games using R15 avatars, among other conditions — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091); [MSN/press](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/roblox-lifts-us-creator-payouts-42-as-2026-forecast-cut/gm-GM08CD8278)

**Regional pricing / Managed Pricing**
- Regional pricing for passes launched Apr 24, 2025 (opt-in), and developer products followed — [Roblox Newsroom Apr 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items); [DevForum: Regional Pricing for Developer Products](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-regional-pricing-for-developer-products/3971235); [DevForum: Enabling regional prices for all passes](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/enabling-regional-prices-for-all-passes-to-grow-your-global-audience/4471199)
- How Managed Pricing works now — [Creator Docs: Managed pricing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing); [Creator Docs: Regional pricing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/regional-pricing):
  - It unifies price optimization (base price) and regional pricing (adjustment by location).
  - New games and items are enrolled by default, with item-level opt-out. Developer products need a separate opt-in because of gifting/trading arbitrage.
  - Regional prices are never discounted more than 70% below the default and never exceed it. VPN users may not get regional prices.
- Roblox claims Price Optimization raised eligible creators' earnings by 4% (company figure) — [Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing)

### Inferences
- **Shooter maturity label:** stylized gun combat without blood probably fits Mild. Realistic weapons or blood push toward Moderate. Moderate appears to exclude the 5–8 "Roblox Kids" pool but keeps 9–15 and 16+. The questionnaire answers directly set your discoverable audience, so design VFX (no blood, stylized hit effects) with the target label in mind.
- **Loot boxes:** avoid Robux-purchasable random boxes entirely, or make every box item earnable and show odds. Direct-purchase cosmetics plus a rotating shop avoids PRI compliance overhead in the UK, Australia and Brazil and stays compliant if more regions follow.
- **Communication:** design for many players lacking chat (not age-checked, or in different age bands). Use pings, quick-chat wheels and emotes rather than relying on text or voice. This matters especially for 2v2/3v3 coordination.
- **Spend targeting:** the US 18+ DevEx bonus and R15 requirement favor R15 avatar support and content appealing to older teens and adults.

### Gaps
- Full text of the May 2026 PRI disclosure requirements, and a complete list of restricted countries, could not be retrieved (roblox.com fetches blocked).
- Exact Creator Rewards formula from official docs not verified; the "35% new-player share" is unverified.
- Full eligibility list for the US 18+ DevEx rate (beyond R15 and age checks) not retrieved.
- Commerce/Creator Store changes for 2026 were not researched within the tool budget.
- Whether "Roblox Kids"/"Roblox Select" are formal product names needs confirmation against live docs.

## 3. Progression: account level, weapon mastery, ranked seasons, ability unlock pacing, quests, streaks

### Takeaway
RIVALS' structure is the reference model:
- Contracts (per-weapon objective challenges → cosmetics)
- Daily login and tasks → Keys
- A 70-tier free/premium season pass driven by wins or playtime
- Seasonal rank resets with non-returning ranked cosmetics paid in a separate Glory currency
- Some new weapons on the free pass track

There is no direct evidence on drafted-ability unlock pacing, so that is an inference.

### Cited Findings
- Per-weapon contracts reward wraps and finishers for eliminations or damage with that weapon. This is weapon mastery without stat gains — [RIVALS Wiki: Contracts](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Contracts)
- Daily login rewards, tasks, codes and a Prize Wheel all pay Keys — [RIVALS Wiki: Currencies](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)
- Season Pass: 70 tiers, with XP from duel-round wins or timed play (e.g., 25 rounds or 20 minutes per step) — [RIVALS Wiki: Season Pass](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Season_Pass)
- Seasons reset rank and the pass, add new weapons, and do not re-release ranked rewards (third-party) — [dungeonpath season guide](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/rivals/complete-season-guide/)
- New weapons are placed on the free pass track (Spear), with another on premium (Grappler, 799 Robux pass) per a third-party guide — [dungeonpath S3](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/rivals/season-3-battle-pass-guide/)
- Ranked milestone rewards: a skin at 200 ranked wins, top-rank exclusive items, and a final-rank charm — [rosenberryrooms](https://www.rosenberryrooms.com/rivals-roblox-season-1-update/)
- Arsenal uses an XP/level system with a group-membership XP bonus (+25%) — [Roblox: Arsenal](https://www.roblox.com/games/286090429/Arsenal)

### Inferences
- **Abilities in a draft format:** in a draft, unequal ability pools undermine competitive integrity, because the draft is the skill expression. Recommended approach:
  - All abilities are free in Ranked.
  - New players get a small curated starter pool in Casual/FTUE so the draft isn't overwhelming, then unlock the full pool quickly (e.g., within the first several matches or account levels).
  - Monetization is cosmetic only (ability VFX skins, finishers, charms).

  RIVALS shows that new weapons can be gated behind free-track pass XP without major backlash. A premium-track-only gameplay item is a P2W risk to avoid.
- Use a separate earned ranked currency (Glory-like) so top players have status items that can't be bought. Seasonal rank resets give recurring goals.
- **Daily and weekly quests** should tie to queue variety (1v1, 2v2, 3v3) and to trying different drafted abilities, which spreads play across modes and keeps queues healthy.
- **Win-or-time pass XP** (RIVALS style) rewards losing players too, which is important for retention in 1v1, where half the players lose every match.

### Gaps
- No public data on how unlock pacing affects retention for Roblox shooters specifically.
- No source on optimal login-streak design for Roblox shooters.

## 4. Roblox discovery / recommendation algorithm (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Since 2025, Roblox's "Recommended For You" ranks experiences on per-user averages of long-term engagement and monetization. A June 15, 2026 update reportedly extended the retention window from 7 to 28 days. It also reportedly replaced qualified play-through rate (qPTR) with play-through rate (PTR) plus first-play bounce rate (a negative signal), and elevated co-play and spend signals. Small games with highly engaged users are not disadvantaged by size.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 DevForum announcement: qPTR defined as engaging plays divided by Recommended-For-You impressions. Signals are computed as per-user averages, not totals, so smaller experiences with engaged users are not penalized — [DevForum: Improved Recommended For You algorithm](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)
- June 15, 2026 update (secondary sources): Recommended For You moved from a 7-day to a 28-day view measuring long-term retention — [zehn-studio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/); [sozai video summary](https://sozai.app/transcript/roblox-algorithm-changes-june-2026/)
- Reported signal tiers after June 2026 — [zehn-studio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/); [gmmarket](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/how-the-roblox-discovery-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026-myths-debunked):
  - "Most Important" includes first-play bounce rate (negative).
  - "Important" includes intentional co-play days, qualified play sessions, spend days and Robux spent, all per user.
  - One source says qPTR was removed and PTR and first-play bounce added. Another still lists "qualified play sessions", so sources partly conflict.
- Roblox framed its discovery changes as anti-clickbait, rewarding games that retain players over those that only win the click — [endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)
- Co-play (friends playing together) is reported to be favored by the new algorithm — [Maxpower Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/why-roblox-s-new-discovery-algorithm-favors-games-that-bring-friends-together)
- Analysis of new-game breakout factors — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off); [moze "four metrics"](https://note.com/moze_inc/n/n7a4090365c9b?hl=en)
- Paid acquisition through Sponsored Experiences in Ads Manager:
  - Advertisers bid cost-per-play and pay only when a player joins from the experience details page.
  - 2023 terms: minimum 10 ad credits (5/day for 2 days), with 1 Ad Credit = 285 Robux. These may be outdated.

  [DevForum: Sponsored Experiences moving to Ads Manager](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756)
- RDC 2025 recap: Roblox claims cost-per-play is "over 71% cheaper" and players can be acquired for "less than $0.01" (company claim) — [DevForum: Ads for Creators RDC Recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)
- Other cost reports conflict:
  - BLOXG (third-party): average $0.45 per play, and elsewhere $0.01–0.05 per visit, which contradicts itself — [BLOXG ad benchmarks](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-advertising-benchmarks); [BLOXG ads guide](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-ads-guide)
  - A developer reported about $8 per 1,000 plays on "Maximize Plays" — [DevForum thread p.10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756?page=10)

### Inferences
- **For a 1v1/2v2/3v3 queue game, the critical metrics are:**
  - First-play bounce rate: players must reach a fun match fast, without long queue waits.
  - 28-day retention: a ranked season and pass cadence supports this.
  - Intentional co-play days: party queue for 2v2/3v3 is algorithmically valuable, not just a social feature.
  - Spend days per user: a daily shop rotation encourages small, frequent purchases.
- **Bots/backfill:** long matchmaking queues at low CCU directly hurt bounce rate. Bots or instant backfill (or a free-for-all/practice lobby while queuing) are likely essential at launch.
- **Paid acquisition:** cheap paid plays only help if they convert to retained users, because signals are per-user averages. Buying low-intent traffic can drag averages down. Use ads after the FTUE is tuned.

### Gaps
- Could not read the official June 2026 DevForum post directly; the exact signal list and qPTR removal are from secondary sources.
- No official data found on thumbnail/icon A/B testing in 2026. Roblox has offered thumbnail personalization and experiment tooling in prior years (training-era knowledge, unverified here).
- No 2026 data on YouTuber/TikTok marketing ROI or official guidance on update cadence.

## 5. Retention benchmarks (D1/D7/D30) and FTUE best practices

### Takeaway
Across 500+ Roblox games with 1M+ MAU (Aug 2025–Jul 2026), median retention is D1 10.3%, D7 1.6% and D30 0.5%. The top 1% reach D1 22.2%, D7 9.1% and D30 4.7%. The popular "20% D1 is good" rule of thumb comes from biased samples. For FTUE, the consensus is to get players into fun gameplay within seconds, delay non-essential UI and prompts, and instrument funnel events.

### Cited Findings
- GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox Benchmark Report — [GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox Report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report); [GameDev Reports summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox):
  - Sample: 500+ games with 1M+ MAU, Aug 2025–Jul 2026.
  - Median: D1 10.3%, D7 1.6%, D30 0.5%.
  - Top 1%: D1 22.2%, D7 9.1%, D30 4.7%.
  - Suggested realistic target band: from median to p75–p90, i.e. D1 of 10.3% to 12.9–15.9%.
- InvestGame's 2025 Roblox report: median D1 near 8% — [InvestGame 2025](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/the-2025-roblox-benchmark-report/)
- BLOXG (850+ promoted games, Mar 2026): average D1 28%, D7 11%, D30 4.5%; top-10% D1 38% and D7 18%. This is a selection-biased sample of already-promoted games — [BLOXG retention by genre](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-retention-benchmarks); [BLOXG marketing stats](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-marketing-statistics-2026)
- Other benchmark compilations — [RRQ World](https://blog.rrqworld.com/roblox-game-retention/); [Maxpower](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/unlock-how-top-roblox-games-actually-perform)
- **FTUE advice from DevForum (anecdotal):**

### Inferences
- **Realistic targets** for a new competitive shooter: D1 at least 12–16% and D7 at least 3–5% would be above median. Approaching D7 of 9% puts it in the top 1%.
- **FTUE for an ability-draft shooter:**
  - Skip or auto-pick the draft for the first match.
  - Put new players into an immediate 1v1 vs a bot (time-to-first-shot under about 10–20 seconds).
  - Introduce drafting in match 2–3 with a limited pool.
  - Unlock ranked after N casual matches.
  - Instrument every step as a funnel event.

## 6. Social features: invites, parties, friend-joining, referrals, spectating, clips

### Takeaway
Roblox's official Friend Referral System (2025) lets any game that has been live for a day reward both inviter and invitee. Invites can reach people who aren't yet Roblox friends, links never expire, and attribution comes via `ReferredByPlayerId` in `GetJoinData()`. Combined with the algorithm's reported co-play weighting and chat now requiring age checks, party and invite systems are high-leverage for 2v2/3v3 queues.

### Cited Findings
- Friend Referral System:
  - Open to any game live at least one day that doesn't violate the Community Standards.
  - Can reward both inviter and invitee, can invite non-friends, and links never expire.
  - Developers can open the invite modal from an in-game button, with a reward banner.

  [Creator Docs: Friend referral system](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/referral-system)
- Attribution is via `ReferredByPlayerId` in `GetJoinData()`. Rewards can be badges, currency or perks shown in an in-experience banner. The announcement is about 1.5 years old — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-the-new-friend-referral-system-for-your-experiences/3623795)
- Roblox Plus subscription referral (separate, platform-level): both users get 100 Robux after the referred subscriber's first payment (Sept 2026 report) — [Rolimons (X)](https://x.com/Rolimons/status/2102540624083988967)
- The algorithm reportedly weights "intentional co-play days" per user — [zehn-studio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/); [Maxpower](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/why-roblox-s-new-discovery-algorithm-favors-games-that-bring-friends-together)
- Chat requires an age check, and cross-band chat is limited — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat)

### Inferences
- **Build party queue first-class:** one-click "invite to party" via the referral modal, a party carried into 2v2/3v3 queues, and invite rewards limited to cosmetics or currency. Pair with non-chat communication (pings, emotes) because chat access is age-gated.
- **Group-join incentive** (Arsenal-style +XP/currency for joining the group) is a cheap way to build a notifiable community.
- **Spectating and kill-cams** in 1v1 duels give eliminated players something to do between rounds and support content-creator clips.

### Gaps
- No 2026 source found on new SocialService party APIs, spectating APIs, or native Roblox clip/capture features for creators (searches returned nothing official).
- No data on referral program uplift for Roblox shooters.
