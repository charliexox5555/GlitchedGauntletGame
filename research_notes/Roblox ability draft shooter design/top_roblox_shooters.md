# Top Roblox Shooters: What Makes Them Successful (as of October 2026)

> Research method note (2026-10-17): In this sandbox, direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) were blocked by the egress proxy for every target (rowatcher.com, romonitorstats.com, bloxodes.com, wikipedia.org, devforum.roblox.com). ## Q1. How does RIVALS structure its lobby, queue, ranked, duels, round format and loadouts, and why did it explode?

### Takeaway
RIVALS (Nosniy Games, released mid-2024) is a social lobby with step-on duel pads. Each pad queues a team size from 1v1 to 5v5, and matches are first to 5 round wins with a 4-slot loadout. It grew into Roblox's #1 shooter by a wide margin: all-time peak estimates run from about 680K to 967K CCU (Feb 2026), and it typically holds 110K-290K CCU in 2026. Its draw is crisp, skill-expressive gunplay in short rounds, plus a steady stream of weapons, cosmetics and ranked.

### Cited Findings
**Identity and timeline**
- RIVALS is a fast-paced PvP shooter by Nosniy Games, whose earlier games include Treasure Quest and Super Golf! — [Roblox Rivals Wiki (Fandom)](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Roblox_Rivals_Wiki)
- Release date conflicts between sources: one says June 28, 2024 and another says May 26, 2024 — [Rivals Fandom: Nosniy Games](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Nosniy_Games); [Rivals Fandom: Updates](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Updates)
- **Design origin:** per the developer notes, Nosniy picked duels because it was the fastest mode to build after a long gap without a release, and the team pivoted from a PvE idea to a fast PvP shooter — [Rivals Fandom: Nosniy Games](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Nosniy_Games)
- Won the RIA25 (Roblox Innovation Awards 2025) "Best Shooter Experience" award — [Rivals Fandom: Nosniy Games](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Nosniy_Games)
- Visits: 13.1B all-time per a March 2026 report — [RoWatcher, Mar 2026 Top 10](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown). A later guide (Oct 2026) says 16.9B+ visits, with "regularly over 200,000 active players" — [Sportskeeda, Best Roblox games Oct 2026](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/best-roblox-games-to-play-right-now)

**Lobby and queue**
- Duel pads sit in public and private lobbies. A duel pad is "a lobby plate you step on to enter matchmaking" — [Rivals Fandom: Mechanics](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanics); [newgame.co.jp RIVALS guide](https://newgame.co.jp/metaverse/en/roblox/17625359962/rivals/guide/)

**Loadouts**
- 4 slots. The free starter is assault rifle, handgun, fists and grenade, roughly primary / secondary / melee / utility — [bloxodes beginner guide](https://bloxodes.com/articles/rivals-beginner-guide)

**Ranked**
- Unlock requirements (Sportskeeda): win 10 duels, reach Level 30, Roblox account at least 14 days old. Then 10 placement matches; ELO sets rank and matchmaking. An "ELO Shield" prevents point loss on a defeat — [Sportskeeda Rivals Ranked guide](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/rivals-ranked-guide)

Free loss shields stop from Platinum I up, and inactivity decay starts at Onyx — [bloxodes ranks](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/ranks). Earned from contracts, tasks, codes and a prize wheel, or bought with Robux — [Rivals Fandom: Keys](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Keys)
- **Weapons:** "Prime" weapons (e.g. Flamethrower, Energy Rifle) are bought with keys. "Contraband" weapons come only from low-drop-rate crates — [Rivals Fandom: Keys](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Keys)
- **Shop:** wraps, skins, charms and finishers for Robux or Keys. Randomized Skin Cases, Wrap Boxes and Charm Capsules. Bundles run about 49-1,149 Robux. The Daily Shop rotates 5 skins; a keys refresh starts at 3 and doubles up to 24 — [Rivals Miraheze: Shop](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Shop); [Rivals Fandom: Skins](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Skins)
- **Glory:** a separate ranked currency for the "Glory Shop" skins — [newgame.co.jp guide](https://newgame.co.jp/metaverse/en/roblox/17625359962/rivals/guide/)
- Fandom says skins mostly need Robux, while guides say good weapons are earnable free. Read together: power is mostly earnable and cosmetics are monetized — [Rivals Fandom: Skins](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Skins); [newgame.co.jp](https://newgame.co.jp/metaverse/en/roblox/17625359962/rivals/guide/)

**Why players praise it**
- One guide calls it "like a high-budget PC shooter crammed into the browser", with gunplay that is "crisp, responsive, and demands actual skill". Sources also credit a constant stream of seasonal weapons and balance changes, plus ranked and cosmetics — [Sportskeeda Oct 2026](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/best-roblox-games-to-play-right-now); [RobloxGameFinder: games like Rivals](https://robloxgamefinder.com/pages/articles/games-like-rivals.html)
- **Creator culture:** many YouTube videos show creators 1v1ing the owner, Nosniy (e.g. "i 1v1d the OWNER of rivals but if i lose I GET BANNED") — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUldYVN0QF8); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OVwtUJuOd3g)

### Inferences
- **Duels made RIVALS fast to build and easy to read.** A duels-first scope, round-based first-to-5 with 1v1 to 5v5 on one ruleset, is cheap to build, easy to understand, and naturally "clip-able" for YouTube and TikTok. A Gauntlet-drafting game can copy this shape: one ruleset across all team sizes.
- **Pads keep queueing social.** Physical pads in a social lobby make queueing visible: you can see who is waiting and challenge friends. This likely helps both social virality and perceived queue health versus a menu button.
- **Ranked is gated against smurfs and cheaters.** Level, wins, account age and later ID verification are used as anti-smurf and anti-cheat friction. Cheap alt accounts are the main Roblox cheating vector, so a new game should gate ranked and tournament modes the same way.
- **The ranked swap cap hints at draft design.** Capping loadout swaps at 2 per duel shows that RIVALS already treats mid-match adaptation as a strategic resource. This maps directly onto between-round ability drafting.
- **Monetization stays cosmetic.** It is cosmetic-heavy (skins, wraps, charms, finishers, cases) with only light power-adjacent purchases. Ad-rewarded keys and weapon trials (June 2026) widen free access.
- **Trend:** CCU is very seasonal (school holidays and update spikes) and runs at roughly 15-30% of the all-time peak on normal days.

## Q2. What do Arsenal, Phantom Forces, Bad Business, Frontlines and others do well (game feel, progression, session length)?

### Takeaway
The legacy shooters each excel at one thing:
- **Arsenal:** a chaotic, short, casual loop.
- **Phantom Forces:** a deep realistic gun sandbox with long per-weapon progression.
- **Bad Business and Frontlines:** high-fidelity "AAA feel" on Roblox.
- **Hypershot (2024-26):** arena movement plus one per-match ability, currently #2.

All but Hypershot are now far smaller than RIVALS.

### Cited Findings
**Arsenal (ROLVe)**
- Created Aug 18, 2015; 6.3B+ visits. Rolimons all-time peak: 152,613 CCU on Oct 26, 2020 — [Rolimons: Arsenal](https://www.rolimons.com/game/286090429). RoMonitor shows a 54.09K all-time peak and about 3.03K average (tracker conflict) — [RoMonitor: Arsenal](https://romonitorstats.com/game/286090429/)
- Event-driven spikes: after Roblox's "Classic" event (2024), Arsenal peaked at 33,000 CCU, 9,346 above its Roblox Hunt peak — [Roblox Arsenal News on X, May 2024](https://x.com/RBX_ArsenalNews/status/1794042675689058517?lang=en)
- About 10K CCU until H2 2023, then about 3K by late 2024 — [NamuWiki: Arsenal](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox)). About 2.7K listed in 2026 — [RobloxDen FPS ranking](https://robloxden.com/best-games/genres/fps)
- An Earnaldo comparison claims 15K-40K CCU for Arsenal. This contradicts the trackers and is likely unreliable — [Earnaldo PF vs Arsenal](https://earnaldo.com/blog/phantom-forces-vs-arsenal)
- "Arsenal Reloaded" released July 2025 — [NamuWiki: Arsenal](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox))

**Phantom Forces (StyLiS Studios)**
- Progression: personal rank from XP (kills, objectives). Credits come from ranking up, selling skins, or real money, and can buy guns outright. Attachments (optics, barrels, underbarrel, ammo) unlock **per weapon** through kills or credits — [IMFDB: Phantom Forces](https://imfdb.org/wiki/Phantom_Forces)
- Feel: distinct recoil patterns, muzzle velocity and damage falloff per weapon; attachments change performance, not just looks — [Earnaldo (promotional, low authority)](https://earnaldo.com/blog/phantom-forces-vs-arsenal). Movement includes sprint, slide, prone and vault (low-authority page) — [search snippet, low quality](https://connect.minco.com/6bZ/531/getEouqyfPhantomForcesRoblox)
- CCU: RoMonitor shows 1.98K average, 3.26K recent peak, 11.79K "all-time peak". That last figure is likely only within RoMonitor's tracking window, since PF was far larger in the 2010s — [RoMonitor: Phantom Forces](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/292439477/). About 2.6K CCU in June 2026, "smaller than it used to pull" — [Lootbar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/best-roblox-shooter-games-2026-rivals-phantom-forces-and-more.html). Still updating, e.g. "Fall Weapon Remodels" — [Rolimons: PF](https://www.rolimons.com/game/292439477)

**Bad Business**
- Rolimons all-time peak: 33,739 CCU on Dec 1, 2020; about 144 players on May 30, 2026 — [Rolimons: Bad Business](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3233893879); [RobloxGo](https://www.robloxgo.com/game/3233893879/Bad-Business-FPS)

**Frontlines**
- A 2023 Call-of-Duty-style showcase made players "disbelieve" it was Roblox. The game developed a cult following of about 1K average daily players (2023) — [Sportskeeda: KreekCraft shares CoD-style FPS](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/news-kreekcraft-shares-call-duty-style-fps-made-roblox-leaves-players-disbelief)

**Hypershot (current #2 shooter)**
- An arena FPS blending "classic arena shooter movement with hero-style abilities": 46 weapons in 5 categories plus **one active ability per match** (e.g. Tailwind speed boost, Photon Shield barrier, Regen Splash heal). Abilities are disabled for the flag carrier in CTF — [newgame.co.jp Hypershot guide](https://newgame.co.jp/metaverse/en/roblox/17516596118/hypershot/guide/)
- Rolimons all-time peak: 101,177 on Jan 31, 2026 — [Rolimons: Hypershot](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17516596118). About 22.2K CCU on Oct 12, 2026 (#47 on Roblox), with an Oct 10 hourly range of 21.5K-40.2K — [RoCodes Hypershot](https://rocodes.gg/codes/hypershot)

### Inferences
- **Feel is table stakes.** Bad Business and Frontlines show that AAA-level fidelity alone does not hold a big audience on Roblox; both stayed niche despite praise. RIVALS pairs strong feel with an easy-to-read format, a social lobby and constant content.
- **Ability systems already work at scale.** Hypershot's success (101K peak) shows that "gunplay + one ability" works on Roblox. The drafting game's twist, drafting *multiple* abilities across rounds, is a real step beyond it.
- **Two progression models to borrow.** Phantom Forces' per-weapon attachment unlocks give long-term grind; RIVALS-style key and cosmetic loops give short-session reward. A ranked draft shooter should keep match power *in-match* (the draft) and put meta-progression into cosmetics and unlocking *what can appear* in the draft pool. This is similar to Overkill's token-unlocked cards (see Q5).

### Gaps
- Arsenal's gun-game rotation mechanics (a kill advances you to the next weapon, ending on a melee/golden-knife kill) were not confirmed by any source this session. This is background knowledge only; verify on the [Arsenal wiki](https://arsenal.miraheze.org/wiki/Main_Page).
- Counter Blox, Gunfight Arena and Weaponry: no reliable 2026 data surfaced.
- No session-length or retention (D1/D7) data for any of these games. Developers do not publish it.

## Q3. What do players commonly complain about in Roblox shooters?

### Takeaway
The recurring pain points are exploiters (aimbot/ESP scripts are sold openly), mobile aim-assist fairness (device spoofing is trivial), client-vs-server hit registration trade-offs, slow bug fixes, and paywalled maps and modes. Hard data on RIVALS-specific complaint volume was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Cheats are sold openly.** Keyless "Rivals Ultimate" aimbot/ESP scripts advertised as "undetected" exist — [itch.io](https://itch.io/blog/1363626/rivals-script-keyless); [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/836457478/message-1)
- **Device checks are client-side and spoofable.**
  - Device type can only be detected on the client, so exploiters can enable mobile-only aim assist. A workaround is to send aim-assist code from the server only after the client reports its device — [DevForum: stop PC players accessing mobile aim assist](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-stop-pc-players-from-accessing-aim-assisted-guns-meant-for-mobile-game-security/2019942)
  - "PC players can make roblox think they're playing on mobile even without exploits." Advice: give mobile helper *features* (e.g. an auto-lock button) rather than raw damage or HP buffs — [DevForum: should I give mobile/console players an advantage](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/should-i-give-mobileconsole-players-a-advantage/2051468)
- **Aim assist "makes it unfair to PC players, especially if your game is movement based"** — [DevForum: Help with aim assist](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/help-with-aim-assist/3802674)
- **Hit-reg trade-off.** Looser server hit-validation makes the game "slightly more vulnerable to exploiters". Having all clients confirm a hit adds latency on weak devices — [DevForum: client-responsive and secure hit registration](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/client-responsive-and-secure-hit-registration/3875405)
- **Arsenal complaints:** slow fixes for serious bugs; post-update glitches (physics, spawns, ladders); nerfs to popular weapons (Hush Puppy); good maps and modes moved to VIP-server-only; a "hiding" feature said to push new players away — [Arsenal Fandom: Downfall of Arsenal](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000228957); [Arsenal Fandom: why people quit](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000228832); [NamuWiki: Arsenal](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox))

### Inferences
- **For a drafted-ability game:**
  - Abilities must be validated server-side (cooldowns, ranges, targets) and never trust client device flags.
  - Ranked and Gauntlet tournaments need account-age, level and ideally verification gates (RIVALS precedent).
  - Use input-based matchmaking or input-specific assists carefully; abilities that auto-target can double as "legal aim assist" for touch players.
- **Paywalling maps and modes is a known churn trigger** (Arsenal). Keep Gauntlet and ranked free.

### Gaps
- No quantified data on queue times, toxicity, or the RIVALS mobile/PC split. Reddit threads did not surface through search, and direct fetches were blocked.
- No data on how Roblox's own anti-cheat (Hyperion/Byfron) affects shooter cheating rates in 2026.

## Q4. Which shooters failed or declined, and why?

### Takeaway
Most 2015-2021 shooter leaders (Arsenal, Phantom Forces, Bad Business) are now at roughly 1-3% of their peaks. Causes include Roblox discovery-algorithm changes, slow and underwhelming updates, bugs and paywalls, and above all competition from RIVALS and Hypershot.

### Cited Findings
- **Bad Business:**
  - Its devs reported a "sharp decline in new players" from mid/late April-May 2023, which they attributed to Roblox algorithm changes cutting home-page exposure, not to lost interest — [DevForum: Roblox's algorithm changes are killing our game](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-algorithm-changes-are-killing-our-game/2417236)
  - CCU fell from a 33.7K peak (2020) to about 144 (May 2026) — [Rolimons: Bad Business](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3233893879)
- **Arsenal:**
  - CCU went from 152.6K (Oct 2020) to about 10K (to mid-2023), about 3K (late 2024) and about 2.7K (2026); growth stalled after the 2020 Summer Update — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/286090429); [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox)); [RobloxDen](https://robloxden.com/best-games/genres/fps)
  - The long-time composer (Campyfire) left ROLVe in July 2025 — [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%84%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90(Roblox))
  - A YouTube "Arsenal is dying... 1700 players left" video; a commenter claims roughly 10 months without updates (unverified) — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=00jyNmemmu0); [YouTube: Rise and Fall of Arsenal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dz3z3zIHlz4)
- **Phantom Forces:** now about 2-3K CCU — [RoMonitor](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/292439477/); [Lootbar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/best-roblox-shooter-games-2026-rivals-phantom-forces-and-more.html)
- **BedWars (Easy.gg; kit-ability PvP, relevant precedent):**
  - About 16K CCU, down 30% over 30 days versus 9% platform-wide — [RoWatcher: Is BedWars dying?](https://rowatcher.com/games/2619619496/bedwars-update/dying)
  - A limited "Hyper Kits" mode gave a +12.4% daily bump — [RoWatcher BedWars](https://rowatcher.com/games/2619619496)
  - About 15-30K typical CCU (June 2026, low-authority source) with weekly Friday updates — [Earnaldo BedWars](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bedwars-free-robux-guide)
  - RoWatcher's "all-time peak 68K on 2026-08-01" is **suspect**: it probably reflects only RoWatcher's tracking window or a re-listed place, since BedWars was far larger in 2021-22.
- **Platform context:** Roblox DAU fell from about 151.5M (Q3 2025) to about 123M (Q2 2026). A source attributes this partly to the age-check rollout — [SQ Magazine Roblox statistics](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/)

### Inferences
- **Decline pattern:** stale core loop, slow patches, paywalled content, and a fresher competitor that captured the YouTube and TikTok meta (RIVALS since 2024).
- **Discovery is fragile.** Algorithm shifts can halve new-user inflow regardless of quality (Bad Business), so a new game needs strong early retention and session metrics to earn recommendations.
- **Rotating "spicy" modes give short bumps.** BedWars' Hyper Kits is the example. A Gauntlet mode can serve as both a core mode and a rotating event hook.

### Gaps
- No verified data on specific shut-down or failed shooter launches in 2025-2026 (e.g. high-budget studio shooters that flopped).
- No reliable 2026 CCU for Counter Blox, Gunfight Arena or Weaponry.

## Q5. Are there existing Roblox games with ability drafting or roguelike upgrades between rounds? How did they do?

### Takeaway
Yes, but they are small. **OVERKILL (Repotted)** is the closest analog: a ROUNDS-style deckbuilder shooter where you pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards per round in teams, duels or FFA, first to 5. It peaked at only about 7-8K CCU and sat around 500 CCU by Sept 2026. Valorant-style agent games exist (e.g. "Valoblox") but are poorly documented. Hypershot (one fixed ability per match) is the most successful ability shooter.

### Cited Findings
- **OVERKILL (Roblox):**
  - Pick 1 upgrade card from 3 at the start of each round; only the losing side gets upgrades. Upgrades stack across rounds. Kills earn Tokens, spent after matches to unlock more cards — [Deltia's Gaming: Overkill beginner's guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/roblox-overkill-a-beginners-guide/)
  - "Fast-paced deckbuilder shooter where you stack upgrades to become stronger each round". Teams, duels or FFA, first to 5 points. Repotted listing: about 474 CCU, 7.3K peak (data as of Sept 19, 2026) — [profitable.app: Overkill](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/overkill)
  - RoWatcher reported a surge to about 7K CCU ("4.6x surge") — [RoWatcher: OVERKILL hits 7K](https://rowatcher.com/news/overkill-hits-7k-players-a-4-6-surge-worth-watching); [RoWatcher: Overkill](https://rowatcher.com/games/7872343282/overkill)
  - A different "OVERKILL" listing (creator One-Shot) shows a 15K peak in June 2026 and 549 online, with 19.8M visits. These may be different games — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/8420998291/overkill)
- **ROUNDS (Landfall, non-Roblox inspiration):** a 1v1 shooter where the round loser picks an upgrade card. Matches run to about 4-5 round wins — [Landfall: ROUNDS](https://landfall.se/rounds); [G2A review](https://www.g2a.com/news/reviews/rounds-review-roguelike-fps-duels-with-massive-replay-value/)
- **Skillshot City (non-Roblox):** a PvP roguelite where players pick random abilities on level-up; round winners get a stronger vote for the next round's modifier — [Wikipedia: Skillshot City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skillshot_City)
- **Valorant-likes on Roblox:** "dozens of community-driven experiences" mimic Valorant (Spike/bomb, agent kits). "Valoblox" has smokes, flashes and walls; Project Delta is cited as sharing "ability-based DNA". This is a low-authority single source with no player data — [oreateai article](https://create.oreateai.com/create/best-valorant-inspired-games-you-can-play-on-roblox-today)
- **Other Roblox examples:**
  - Monster Slayer (2023) is a roguelike bullet hell with 24 in-game upgrades/skills (PvE) — [DevForum: Monster Slayer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monster-slayer-a-rogue-like-bullet-hell-game/2512465)
  - Purgatory (Roblox) has an upgrades system — [Purgatory wiki: Upgrades](https://purgatory-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrades)
  - Crazy Cards (Roblox) is a card-based game — [Roblox: Crazy Cards](https://www.roblox.com/games/11945996815/Crazy-Cards)

### Inferences
- **The niche is validated but unproven at scale.** Per-round card/ability drafting works mechanically on Roblox (OVERKILL), but no draft shooter has broken out. OVERKILL's spike-then-fade (about 7K to about 500) suggests novelty alone doesn't retain.
- **Likely missing pieces for a breakout,** inferred from RIVALS:
  1. Crisp core gunplay first, with abilities layered on top.
  2. A social pad lobby with 1v1 to 5v5.
  3. Ranked with integrity gates.
  4. A cosmetic economy.
  5. A creator-friendly format (clip-worthy ability combos).
  6. A frequent content cadence (new abilities each update is a natural hook).
- **"Only losers pick" (OVERKILL/ROUNDS) is a built-in comeback mechanic,** good for short 1v1s. A team Gauntlet tournament may want both teams to draft, with losers getting extra picks or rerolls, so snowballing doesn't end matches early.
- **Unlock rules shape fairness.** Token-unlocked cards (OVERKILL) tie meta-progression to the draft pool. That risks a pay- or grind-to-win perception in ranked. Consider all cards being available in ranked, with unlocks limited to casual or cosmetic variants.

### Gaps
- No confirmed Deadlock-style or Valorant "Gauntlet: Glitched"-style drafting shooter on Roblox with public player counts.
- OVERKILL's identity and figures conflict across trackers (Repotted vs One-Shot listings).
- No reliable CCU for Valoblox or other Valorant-clone experiences.

## Q6. What are the typical audience demographics and devices for Roblox shooters?

### Takeaway
Roblox's audience is mostly teens and kids: about 73% under 18 among age-checked users in 2026. 18+ is the fastest-growing segment. Mobile is the dominant device (an often-cited, dated figure of about 72% of activity). No shooter-specific demographic data was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Age:** age-checked users (2026) are about 35% under 13, 38% 13-17 and 27% 18+. Roblox moved from self-reported ages to verified age checks in 2026, so the data is not comparable with older figures — [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/); [Paysfair: Who plays Roblox](https://paysfair.com/blog/who-plays-roblox/)
- **18+:** reportedly growing more than 50% YoY and spending about 40% more per session (citing a CEO shareholder letter; secondary source) — [Skillademia](https://www.skillademia.com/statistics/roblox-statistics/)
- **Gender:** about 51% male / 44% female, or 52% / 39% / 9% unspecified, depending on source — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/how-many-people-play-roblox/); [ShaneTheGamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/roblox-statistics/)
- **Devices:** mobile about 72% of activity, PC about 25%, console about 3%. Low-authority sources; likely pre-2026 — [Takeaway Reality](https://www.takeaway-reality.com/post/roblox-demographics-statistics); [Prioridata](https://prioridata.com/data/roblox-users/)
- **Platform scale:** 111.8M DAU in Q2 2025 — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1220905/roblox-most-visited-games). Peak of about 151.5M DAU in Q3 2025, falling to about 123M in Q2 2026 — [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/). A Roblox record of 47.3M concurrent users was reported — [ResetEra thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/roblox-hit-47-3-million-concurrent-users-the-highest-ever-recorded-on-a-gaming-platform-beating-steam%E2%80%99s-41-2-million-peak-in-march.1281276/)

### Inferences
- **Design mobile-first.** Treat touch as a first-class input: large ability buttons, readable draft UI on small screens, and short decision timers suited to touch. Most players will probably be on phones even in a "competitive" shooter.
- **Clarity and safety matter.** The core audience is 13-17, which favors fast, readable, low-text ability choices (icons, simple tooltips), short matches, and age-appropriate cosmetics and comms.
- **Older players are worth serving.** The growing 18+ segment is the natural audience for a ranked Gauntlet tournament and spends more. Verification gates for ranked also line up with Roblox's 2026 age-check direction.

### Gaps
- No shooter-specific device split (e.g. RIVALS mobile vs PC share) or demographic data. Developers have not published it, and search found none.
- No authoritative 2026 device-share figure from Roblox's own filings surfaced.
