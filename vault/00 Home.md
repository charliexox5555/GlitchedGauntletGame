# 🏠 Home

**Game title:** Draft Duels
**One-liner:** Run-and-gun duels (1v1, 2v2 and 3v3) where you **draft abilities ("Mods") every round** and the shared weapon tier increases as the match progresses. Scheduled 16-player Gauntlet events come later.
