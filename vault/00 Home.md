# 🏠 Home

**Game title:** Draft Duels
**One-liner:** Run-and-gun duels (1v1, 2v2 and 3v3) where you **draft abilities ("Mods") every round** and the shared weapon tier increases as the match progresses. Scheduled 16-player Gauntlet events come later.

---

## 📍 Status

- **First-match flow pass (2026-11-25, from the onboarding funnel).** Final-kill replay removed: a 2.5 s beat on the last kill, then the win cutscene. Beginner gaps between rounds shortened (pick 9 s, round end 2.5 s: kill → next fight ~11.7 s, was ~16–20 s). A new player's first match is first to 3. Their first 2 Beginner matches start instantly in-server (Match 2 no longer queues). No-rematch results screen 13 s (was 20 s). Back in the lobby with the free case, the Backpack opens on it by itself. New `MatchLeft` event (phase, score + last round, first/mode) shows where players leave. Studio: 1-round and Beginner bot matches checked; the new-save-only parts (instant Match 2, Backpack auto-open, first to 3, 13 s results) still need a fresh account or live check. [Match Flow](01%20Design/Match%20Flow.md)

- **Loading and scope fixes (2026-11-25).** A round no longer goes live until each player's own client confirms its fighter is on screen with floor under it (`BodyReady` ack, up to `BODY_READY_WAIT = 12 s`; the select won't skip early meanwhile), so slow loads stop starting as a void camera spectating bots. Scoped snipers: the reflective circle and see-through Mod walls came from the Glass `ScopeLens` (Roblox draws Glass even when hidden, and hides see-through things behind it); gun parts are now flat SmoothPlastic. Skin lights and particles (Sundial, Riftforge) and the show-while-aiming crosshair also stay off under the scope. Lint, build and Studio checks pass (1v1 bot match over 3 rounds; Sundial and Riftforge snipers scoped); a live slow-device test is pending.
