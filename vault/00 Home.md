# 🏠 Home

**Game title:** PowerDuels
**One-liner:** Run-and-gun duels (1v1, 2v2 and 3v3) where you **draft abilities ("Mods") every round** from your own power deck and fight with the gun loadout you picked in the Locker (round 1 sidearms only). Scheduled 16-player Gauntlet events come later.

## 📍 Status

- **Movement feel (2026-11-27, Studio-tested, not published).** A jump out of the automatic run used to stay "Sprint" in the air, so most jumps had no landing dip, air crosshair or air FOV; running jumps are "Air" now (run speed kept), with a small takeoff coil and the landing dip, and a stair step no longer counts as a jump. Running jumps now get the air spread (as designed). Dash Ghost afterimages are pooled (0 clones after the first dash, was ~216 per dash). Owner calls: slide-jump carry 0.8 → 0.9, sprint FOV +4 → +6, and a real-controller check. [Movement Feel 2026-11-27](01%20Design/Movement%20Feel%202026-11-27.md).

- **Loading and map load hardening (2026-11-27, Studio-tested, not published).** Wave 2: a fighter can no longer stay frozen by a leftover floor hold; a late spawn is hittable at once (the body check runs after setup); the loading screen closes up to 0.5 s sooner; a match to the end returned cleanly to the lobby (no hang). 21 match starts in Studio (8 cold reserved-server starts on 7 maps, 13 pad duels in a row in one server): 20 clean, and the only failure was a cause now fixed. Fixed: the voted map loads before round 1's select opens (a draft error used to keep it from loading); a failed map load keeps the old map instead of leaving none; a listed player who reaches the reserved server early now plays instead of standing in its lobby; our DRAFT DUELS teleport screen is handed over the moment a match is found (the bay lift used to hand it over after the 2 s teleport had started, so Roblox's plain screen showed); map vote pictures preload. New client lines `[Health] C map here` / `map content` trace a live "map didn't load". Per-map costs, routed findings (PlayerGui ~8,500 instances, old LockerV2 shipped, range bots ticking on match servers) and the phone test: [Loading and Performance](04%20Tech/Loading%20and%20Performance%202026-11-27.md)

- **Code audit (2026-11-27, Studio smoke-tested, not published).** All 516 Luau files checked. No exploits, dupes, data loss or charge-without-grant: remotes and saves are solid. 34 fixes, most where a match's copied services meet shared code. Pad-duel teardown runs on MatchHost's own thread (arena and slot freed, the trip home can't be killed). Rollers no longer roll 2x speed. A tied error-forfeit no longer gives team 2 the win. Ended matches' hooks drop out of shared lists (memory grew with uptime). The client boots survive one broken controller. Leaving a match restores mouse speed, reset and chat, and clears leaked effect folders and blur. The global queue falls back to local matches when MemoryStore is down. Studio: lobby, a bot match to the end, teardown, Locker and Shop, no console errors. Parked and routed items: Wave 2: a measured pass on the client perf list (only the Grip part search was a real cost, now throttled), match bots take damage only from their own match, one Crash Recovery hook per match, range bots idle when nobody can see the range (41 bots, 0 moving in a hosted match), and the `game.Loaded` wait before MatchReady measured at 0 s (the hosted gap is the match client's boot). [Code Audit](04%20Tech/Code%20Audit%202026-11-27.md)

- **Phone + controller UI check (2026-11-27, Studio-tested, not published).** The match scorebug no longer overlaps Roblox's buttons or Settings: it measures what sits beside it and shrinks to fit, and a 3v3 bar on a phone moves under Roblox's row. On touch, Settings joins the scoreboard/inspect row, which no longer slips under Roblox's menu bar after a device change. Locker v3 keeps out of the notch and from under Roblox's buttons. Every lobby page keeps the controller selection inside it, and B steps back (Locker: confirm, then the skin strip). The draft focuses a card on a pad. Kill-feed and round-banner phone spots were changed in Studio: **save the place**. Loading: lobby-only screens now live in `ServerStorage.LobbyUI` (match servers never get them; match PlayerScripts 5,656 → 1,910), the Shop and Battle Pass build on first open (lobby PlayerGui at join 6,281 → 3,091), and the Locker stage is sent to lobby players only. Audit wave 2: the lobby HUD no longer shows over a pad duel started with the Locker open; template waits time out instead of hanging a screen; per-frame UI cut (resting health panel 10 → 0.8 µs, off-screen lobby guns don't turn, at most 2 onlooker case openings). Open: small lobby HUD text on phones (5-6 px). [UI Device Check](02%20Art/UI%20Device%20Check%202026-11-27.md)

- **Economy pass (2026-11-27, config only, not published).** Gun set 10,500 coins: Prowler, Sheriff and Shorty 400 for the first unlock (affordable after match 1 with the 500 starting coins; the Range tasks pay 500 more after match 2), up to Deadeye 1,800. Match pay is 10 to finish and +40 for a win (×0.6 vs bots, cap 600/day), and the Task Path pays 16,375 coins (Recruit 1,900). Coin packs, the pack price, login and the pass are unchanged. Logic still to add: coins per round and per kill, a first-win bonus, no pay for idling, a same-opponent cap. [Shop Economy → Economy pass](01%20Design/Shop%20Economy%20and%20Art%20v1.md#economy-pass-earn-vs-spend-hours-to-each-goal-2026-11-27)

- **Onboarding for the loadout update (2026-11-27, Studio-tested, not published).** The first two matches now play the real loadout: Classic in round 1, Havoc from round 2, nothing handed out. Both are **first to 4** (owner: "4, maybe 5"; bump match 1 with `Config/Onboarding SCRIPT[1].roundsToWin`). Match 2 is the real draft. Back in the lobby the Locker gets an **UNLOCK** badge once they can afford their first gun (500 starting coins, so after match 1) and opens on it (replaces the old case/equip steps); after match 2 the Range unlocks with the first NEW. **Onboarding matches leave the hub** (owner): a reserved server at once with bots, Studio-only in-server. `HANDED_GUNS`, the weapon-phase boost and first to 3 are gone. Not seen: the real teleport, phones. [First Ten Minutes §13](01%20Design/First%20Ten%20Minutes.md#13-onboarding-for-the-loadout-update-2026-11-27)

- **SURGE PACK: coins only (2026-11-27, Studio-tested, not published).** 1,500 coins a pack, 4,500 for three, for everyone (owner: coins only, no Robux product; the game is rated for paid random items, so no PolicyService gate). Tickets no longer buy packs; never more packs than unowned powers; a pack with nothing left pays 1,500 coins. Shop popup: BUY x1 / BUY x3 (new Studio button `CasePanel.BuyCoins3`: **save the place**). [Loadout and Power Deck → step 4](01%20Design/Loadout%20and%20Power%20Deck.md#launch-plan-for-the-loadout-update-2026-11-27)

- **Gun set + balance pass: Hornet and Scout back, TwinShot gone (2026-11-27, not published).** The loadout is now 10 primaries + 4 secondaries. **Hornet** (Stinger, 600 coins) is the fastest trigger (17.2/s, 0.70 s close kill) with a ton of kick and steep falloff; **Scout** (Marshal, 1,000) is the quick sniper: fastest to move with, accurate from the hip, head + body kills. **TwinShot (Outlaw) is removed** from the code and Studio (model, 10 skins, wall displays); the pass's Frostbite TwinShot is now a Frostbite Marksman; old saves load clean. Balance: **Lancer is a 3-round burst rifle**, Prowler 13 a round, Mauler 14, Pump 7 a pellet; measured TTK table by range in [Gun Balance](01%20Design/Gun%20Balance.md). Studio bot matches: Locker unlock/equip, round 2 hands out and fires Hornet and Scout. Not yet felt in hand (Hornet kick, Lancer bursts). **Owner: save the place** (Outlaw models deleted in Studio).

- **Skin cases: only skins for guns you own; no onboarding case (2026-11-27, not published).** Cases roll only skins for the knife and active guns you own (Havoc + Classic for a new player), odds recalculated per player; the CONTENTS list says "Only skins for guns you own" and refreshes on a gun unlock; a case with nothing left for your guns isn't sold. The daily shop shows active guns only (not owned-only). The free Starter Case after match 1 is gone (owner: too strong, RIVALS doesn't), with its auto-open, callouts and the admin JUMP TO FREE CASE; old unopened ones still open.
