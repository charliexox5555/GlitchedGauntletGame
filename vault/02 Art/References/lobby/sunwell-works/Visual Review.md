# Sunwell Works picture review

## Pre-flight

- Mirror the structure across the arrival axis. Align the two pairs of doorway centers and balance their visual weight.
- Put the Gauntlet turbine at the end of the centerline and keep the promenade open.
- Give each sign its own silhouette and lettering treatment while keeping the word readable at player distance.
- Use Roblox-scale forms, matte surfaces and a small number of purposeful bevels. Match one view at spawn and another looking back.
- Evaluate only evidence in the images. The Studio walk, collision and performance gates belong to the first build.

## Final image score

Three independent blind picture reviewers graded the final arrival, reverse and lettering sheets. The score is the weighted median per criterion, 22/24 points. [Project World rubric](../../../../01%20Design/Maps/Map%20Quality%20Standard.md#6-the-review-loop)

| Criterion | Weight | Reviewer 1 | Reviewer 2 | Reviewer 3 | Median |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Composition and focus | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Navigation and landmarks | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| Style cohesion | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| Lighting and atmosphere | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Detail balance | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Avatar scale | 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |

The remaining median deduction is for continuity across the sheets. The Duels slash count and Shop trim vary in close-up. The lettering sheet shows a deeper Range recess than Phase 1. The build brief fixes the mounts and uses the hall views for the closed gate treatments. One reviewer flagged the reverse chevrons pointing toward an entry that appears capped, and another wanted avatar scale confirmed at the thresholds. The prompt calls for route checks and R15 tests in Studio.

Studio collision, camera behavior, phone readability and performance belong to the staged build review.
