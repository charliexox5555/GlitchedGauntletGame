## Recon bow and sensor bolt

[Concept sheet](Recon%20Bow%20and%20Sensor%20Bolt.png)

Create one wide, landscape game prop concept sheet for the stylized Roblox competitive shooter Glitched Gauntlet. It is a practical visual reference for a Blender modeler. A clean pale warm-gray studio background, gentle contact shadows, crisp readable planes, neutral white light. Show an original compact RECON BOW and its SENSOR BOLT. One large clear three-quarter near-side view of the complete bow is the dominant subject; to the right include a smaller orthographic side profile and a larger isolated side view of the matching sensor bolt. Space the views comfortably, leaving all extremities visible. Compact tactical recurve silhouette assembled from six to twelve distinctly flat angular plates with carved, chamfered tips, stepped limb bases and a dark recessed hand grip, a visually taut thin straight bowstring, short sight window. Simple enough to build as separate animated limbs, string, grip and nocked projectile. A narrow cool-cyan charge strip along the riser and exactly two small separately readable cyan bounce indicator pips by the grip. Matte charcoal, light neutral composite plates, restrained cyan accents, no haze obscuring shapes. The bolt has three small geometric fins and an angular inset cyan sensor tip, clearly a scanning device. Chunky but precise, a cross between clean tactical stylization and Roblox readability. Readable faceted construction, elegant balance, large shapes and two or three smaller details. No character, arrows in flight, branding, pseudo-text, busy blueprint annotations, luminous outlines, purple glitch motifs, realistic military detail, or replication of any existing game's asset. Use no text.

## Molly and Frost canisters

[Concept sheet](Molly%20and%20Frost%20Canisters.png)

Create a wide landscape side-by-side concept sheet for two hand-held Roblox ability props, for the Blender modeler of Glitched Gauntlet, a stylized competitive shooter. Clean pale warm-gray studio background, even neutral white light, soft contact shadows. One large 3/4 hero view for each prop side-by-side with clear negative space, and a smaller alternate flat side silhouette view directly below each. Match scale between props. Left: MOLLY, a short broad three-faceted incendiary canister with dark charcoal collar, warm burnt-orange broad trapezoid panel and a small amber fuse window; a bold broad chamfer on the shoulder and indentation where the thumb goes. Right: FROST, a taller narrower faceted cryo canister with broken white ceramic-looking matte plates, graphite ribs, a few generously cut cooling vents with a subdued cold-cyan interior, and a tapered crystalline-angled shoulder. Use a common recognizable cap latch and grip family but give each a distinctly different silhouette and value grouping when held in the first-person hand. Both are playful original tactical game gadgets made of clean flat faces, visible large-bevel cuts, stepped collars and tapered ends. Flat shading with large faces, simplified engineered construction, matte low-specular materials, readable at thumbnail size, no cylinders with smooth surfaces, no rounded blob forms or plain boxes. No hands, scene effects, particle trails, complex decals, photorealistic wear, purple neon, brand marks, text, overlay typography, or unrelated props.
