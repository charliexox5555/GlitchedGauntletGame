# Prompt for ChatGPT: Arcade (Havoc concept)

Season 1 battle pass, Rare line (small shape changes in parts). One chat for the whole line, so ChatGPT remembers the look. The build: [Arcade and Frostbite Handoff](../Arcade%20and%20Frostbite%20Handoff.md).

**Attach first:** `art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Vandal_model_sheet.png` (the Havoc's model sheet).

```
I'm designing weapon skins for my own Roblox first-person shooter. The attached image is the base model sheet of our rifle, the Havoc: every angle, its magazine, its sights, its charm mount, and its colour roles.

Skin line: ARCADE. A retro arcade cabinet turned into a gun skin, for a battle pass. It will go on every gun in the game, so the look must be a system that carries across rifles, SMGs, shotguns, snipers and pistols, not a one-off.

Mood: neon arcade at night, 80s/90s cabinet side art, playful but clean and premium. Bold, readable from far away.

Look:
- Cabinet body: a deep glossy colour (try deep purple or midnight blue) with bold flat cabinet side-art shapes: angled stripes, a starburst, pixel steps.
- Bright neon accents (magenta, cyan, yellow) as edge strips and a few glowing panels.
- One pixel-screen panel on the left side of the receiver (the side the player sees), showing a simple pixel graphic (a pixel star or a little ship, no text).
- Signature details that change the shape a little: a coin-slot plate with a glowing "INSERT" bar (shapes only, no letters) on the left of the receiver, and arcade buttons (2 or 3 round, chunky, faceted) set into the side or the grip, plus a small joystick-ball-style top on the charging handle.

Rules:
- This is a "Rare" skin: the gun keeps its silhouette and proportions, and a few parts change shape (coin-slot plate, buttons, a cabinet-style stock end, a screen panel). The outline changes by no more than about a tenth.
- Nothing in the rear sight window or on the line to the front sight. Nothing on the magazine release or trigger.
- Stylised low-poly, faceted: no smooth round blobs (round things are faceted with a few flat sides), flat colour areas, crisp clean edges, small bevels. Like a premium stylised shooter, never photoreal, never cartoon-mushy.
- No real brand logos, no readable text anywhere.
- An original design: don't copy any existing game's skin.

Make it as a model sheet matching the base sheet's layout, on a light grey background:
- large three-quarter view from the front left
- left side, right side and top
- the magazine out of the gun
- a close-up of the coin-slot plate and buttons, and a close-up of the charm mount
- a row of colour swatches labelled with their role (Body, Panel, Dark, Grip, Metal, Accent, Neon) and hex values

Keep every part exactly where it is on the base sheet.
```

**If you want options first:** before that prompt, send *"Before drawing, give me three different colour directions for this ARCADE line (hex per role, one signature detail each) and pick one"*. Then send the prompt with "Go with direction N".
