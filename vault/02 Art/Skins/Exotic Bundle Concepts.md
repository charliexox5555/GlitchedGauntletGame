# Glitched Gauntlet exotic bundle concepts

Three bundles with **six guns, one original melee, and one gun charm each**. The 18 gun variants cover all 17 stock guns. Phantom gets separate Kilnlock and Stacklight variants. The first pass covers 24 concepts and Blender previews. [Unattended concept handoff](Exotic%20Bundle%20Concept%20Handoff.md)

## Spindrift

**Owner revision, 2026-11-18.** The next Kuronami-inspired hero follows the new [Tempest-Strike brief](TempestStrike/concept.md) and [Vandal handoff](TempestStrike/Build%20Handoff.md). The Spindrift roster, concepts and builds below remain the record of the earlier pass.

**Kuronami reference.** Keep the crisp profile and make the water read as a deliberate part of the motion. Koko Liem's design sheet is the best still reference for clean first-person surfaces and the iterations around the water. Kudos shows the difference between rifle, compact, scoped, and melee effects. [Original design pictures](https://kokoliem.artstation.com/projects/aoA4lq) · [Kuronami VFX and animation reel](https://kudos3d.artstation.com/projects/ZavEO8) · [Kuronami 2.0 motion reel](https://kudos3d.artstation.com/projects/0aYweV?album_id=8309122)

**Visual direction.** A storm instrument with a dark basalt shell `#202B36`, folded pearl-alloy cheeks `#CADADD`, sea-glass water `#76E6DA`, and one faded red-orange cuff `#D47C61`. Put a narrow smoked channel between the silver cheeks. Two recessed shutter leaves and one ribbon of water make the line recognizable.

| Gun | Where to put the detail and motion |
| --- | --- |
| **Vandal · hero** | Long two-leaf channel on the player-facing side. Keep the stock nose, ring sight, grip, and rear outline recognizable. The reload gets the full ribbon beat. |
| Spectre | Short paired shutters over the compact side panel. Use a shorter ribbon and keep the suppressor visually quiet. |
| Ghost | One slim shutter below the slide line. Use a brief water wipe during the stock recover beat. Keep the moving slide and suppressor readable. |
| Marshal | Narrow channel below the stock optic. Use a small ripple at each committed stock reload step and save the two-leaf flourish for inspect. |
| Guardian | Stretch the pearl cheek and smoked channel across the fixed body. A small bead follows the channel at reload commit, with the larger shutter reveal reserved for inspect. |
| Bucky | Separate the dark pump treatment from a short, fixed pearl-side channel. Use a small ripple with each committed shell reload step and check clearance through the full stock pump motion. |

**Reload and inspect.** The reload opens with both decorative leaves parting. A narrow tide ribbon sweeps back along the shell during the main stock reload beat, then slips into the smoked channel as the leaves settle at the stock ready cue. A new forearm pose and slight turn can help reveal the channel on the Vandal. Inspect lingers on the slit and lets one suspended bead roll through. The stock gameplay timing remains the anchor for both motions.

**Melee · Sounding Arc.** A hollow crescent with two separated pearl-alloy segments joined visually by a ribbon of water. The grip sits off center under the crescent like a storm instrument handle. Use independent roots for the handle, the two crescent segments, and the water ribbon guide. On equip, the segments sweep out from beside the handle and snap into the floating crescent. In idle, they hold their shape with a restrained drift. The alternating lights are a rising outward sweep and a reverse inward sweep. The heavy move gathers both segments into a broad forward crescent and then reforms the idle shape. In inspect, the player turns the crescent face-on, guides one bead around it, and lets the water drain back into the grip. Draw the closed, open, and gathered silhouettes and the cue for the water ribbon in each state.

**Charm · Stormglass Buoy.** A miniature rounded navigation float with a red-orange cap and one vertically framed sea-glass insert. Show it at rest and with a single bubble rising in the glass. Finish with an oval mount ring and a readable opaque silhouette at icon size.

## Kilnlock

**Prelude to Chaos reference.** Study how layered plates frame a clear power center and how the decorative motion, hand pose and sound sell weight together. Use the artist stills for the shape breaks and the VFX reel for the active state. [Rabcat weapon and chroma pictures](https://rabcat_gameart.artstation.com/projects/5B2Vd1?album_id=705901) · [Gage Burcham's weapon VFX reel](https://gburcham.artstation.com/projects/3ErvKJ)

**Visual direction.** A portable foundry with blackened iron `#282525`, matte firebrick ceramic `#D5C1A4`, oxidized copper `#9A5137`, and a small persimmon-orange heat core `#FF793F`. Use heavy rectangular plates, broad ceramic faces, and offset rectangular vents. The fixed shell and opening cage should read in silhouette before the heat lights up.

| Gun | Where to put the detail and motion |
| --- | --- |
| **Odin · hero** | A low rectangular kiln cage on the fixed left body. Two jaws open to reveal a heat bar. Keep the stock feed, adjacent moving parts, and hand space clear. |
| Bulldog | One side hatch and a compact heat bar on the fixed shoulder. Check the repaired receiver and bolt area during every pose. |
| Shorty | A shallow ceramic-lined hatch on the fixed body. One glow beat follows the stock break-action reload. |
| Sheriff | A small fixed shroud vent opens onto a heat block. Keep the cylinder's original volume and show the vent movement clear of its reload pose. |
| Phantom | Use two wide ceramic panels and a low, offset heat cassette on the fixed side. A short paired jaw reveal follows the Phantom's reload commit. Keep the stock suppressor and sight path clear. |
| Judge | Build a broad ceramic body panel above the drum with a small enamel heat-mark accent on the drum. The rectangular jaws belong to the fixed body, and the heat pulse follows the reload beat. |

**Reload and inspect.** Begin with a short weighted turn. At the existing commit beat the ornamental jaws open, an inner heat bar indexes, and a narrow orange seam flashes. The jaws seat as the stock ready cue lands. Inspect opens the cage slowly for a longer view of the ceramic and hot insert. A low mechanical thump and a soft ceramic click sell the weight.

**Melee · Kiln Cleaver.** A broad two-handed foundry slab with a clipped iron face, pale ceramic jaws, a rear grip, and a distinct support grip. The side jaws and visible heat insert get their own pivots. Draw from low in the frame, catch with the support hand, and seat the jaws. Design a grounded low idle and a compact carry pose. The lights alternate a short diagonal sweep and a broad returning sweep. The heavy uses a slow overhead lift and a downward drop, with a narrow seam flare at the existing contact cue. Inspect turns the slab sideways, opens the jaws in sequence, shows one amber bar, and closes them with a heavy settle. Show grip anchors and both hands at the key poses.

**Charm · Foundry Stamp.** A square ceramic seal with two copper jaws around a small orange square. The hero view shows the jaws apart. The production model can start in the closed pose, with the hinge and glow surface available for one brief reload accent later.

## Stacklight

**RGX 11z Pro reference.** Use the separation between the visible internal hardware and the outer shell, and the way a coordinated light change can connect the skin to its accessory. The Phantom concept pictures show the finish distribution. Riot's trailer and the Kudos reel show lighting and moving components. [Bingxu Liu's Phantom concept pictures](https://jeremy6.artstation.com/projects/JvEEBR) · [Riot's RGX trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O3InzdUOhxs) · [Kudos VFX reel](https://kudos3d.artstation.com/projects/rJOaJG?album_id=8309122)

**Visual direction.** A modular electronics test bench in off-white molded polymer `#E4E1D7`, graphite `#20232E`, smoked blue acrylic `#344656`, and cool mint `#53E4D2`. Use one translucent window with three squared indicator cells and a short clear shuttle. Small lilac and amber accents can become later lighting presets. Start with mint as the shared gun and charm color.

| Gun | Where to put the detail and motion |
| --- | --- |
| **Phantom · hero** | One long dark window and a shallow shuttle track below it. Preserve the stock suppressor and sight picture. Put the three light cells where the held camera can see them. |
| Stinger | A compact three-cell strip above the front grip. Put the moving insert behind the panel and review the repaired handguard join. |
| Classic | Two tiny lit cells with a single short pulse. Keep the fixed accent and the moving slide visually separate. |
| Outlaw | One short strip on the fixed body and a two-step test sweep following the stock reload beats. Preserve the scope picture and action seam. |
| Ares | Fit a wider instrument window to the fixed shoulder. Keep the large three-cell sweep separate from the drum's small status light. |
| Operator | Lay one long diagnostic strip on the fixed body below the optic, with a short clear shuttle near the held camera. Preserve the stock scope image and bolt clearance. |

**Reload and inspect.** The light cells briefly dim as the cosmetic shuttle draws back. A smooth three-cell sweep advances on the stock commit cue, and the shuttle returns flush at ready. Inspect runs a short diagnostic sweep and briefly shows a clean alternate light palette. Color comes from the bundle preset. Any match statistic display is an optional later feature after the hardware read is strong.

**Melee · Patch Tool.** An angular electronics paddle formed by three overlapping translucent panels with an opaque rim and an offset ivory grip. Use separate controls for the nested panel assembly and sliding cheeks. On equip, the outer panels fan out and seat with three ascending ticks. Idle holds the shape on a diagonal with one steady mint indicator. The two light attacks use a quick side snap and a backhand return with different timing and panel poses. For heavy, the panels nest into a narrow baton profile during the windup, expand on the follow-through, and return to the open idle. Inspect turns the tool face-on, opens the handle cheeks for a three-cell diagnostic sweep, then closes them. Visual profile changes use the same gameplay contact window.

**Charm · Keycap Tester.** A single ivory keycap on a dark translucent frame, with one mint underglow. Use an original three-bar glyph. A pressed pose and an illuminated pose give the animator clear reference. Match the equipped Stacklight preset when integrated.

## From concept to production

1. **First art queue.** Make Spindrift Vandal, Kilnlock Odin, and Stacklight Phantom hero sheets in that order, one default finish per line. For each, use its Gauntlet stock render for scale and position, and show three-quarter, left profile, held, applicable stock aim, material close-up, and three illustrated reload beats. Include the charm at reward-icon size and mounted in the held view. Follow with the three original melee pose sheets, then charm sheets and the rest of the roster. Use the self-reviewed heroes as style anchors. [Vandal stock sheet](../../../art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Vandal_model_sheet.png) · [Odin stock sheet](../../../art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Odin_model_sheet.png) · [Phantom stock sheet](../../../art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Phantom_model_sheet.png) · [Knife scale reference](../../../art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Knife_model_sheet.png)
2. **Animation handoff.** For each gun, capture the stock start, commit, ready, and interruption states. Storyboard original hand poses and cosmetic changes against those events. For each melee, block original equip, idle, carry, two light-attack sequences, heavy, inspect, return, and cancel. Design its geometry, grip, rig, and motion from scratch. Validate screen coverage and contact timing against the current game, using the stock melee solely as a reference for scale and gameplay. Give each moving cosmetic piece its own name and pivot. Mark travel and resting pose in the sheet. Prepare cue locators for effects and a separate charm mount. Existing stock sheets expose a charm mount where `CharmPoint` is present. Verify it per host and place it on a fixed exterior area clear of aim, hands, and animation paths.
3. **Blender build.** Deliver clean rest poses, separate rigid animated pieces, material masks, hand and sight comparisons, and inspect/reload pose previews. Capture both sides, the held view, the stock aim view, and a backface-culled reimport of each changed model. Carry through the [Bulldog and Stinger repaired sources](Full%20Roster%20Reskin%20Build%20Ledger.md) and rerun the [geometry regression checks](Reskin%20Geometry%20Regression%20Checks.md).
4. **Later integration.** Keep reload and equipment readiness, ammo updates, accuracy, melee contact, and aim aligned with stock. Reset cosmetics on cancel, unequip, or player reset. Scope the full flourishes to the local viewmodel and use a settled world model. Keep stock gameplay audio cues audible. First prove one skin and charm through equip, reload, inspect, cancel and aim before wiring a roster or any menus. [Riot's skin design and readability notes](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-craft-and-fantasy-of-valorant-weapon-skins/)

Custom flash, trace color, audio accents, and larger end-of-round effects can be explored once the hero silhouettes and motion read. Use restrained local water, heat, and digital accents in the first prototypes. The supplied research PDF also discusses Ruination, progression, emotes, and finishers. Those are reference ideas for future scope. The current production proposal is the three named bundles and their modeled cosmetics, charms, and animation briefs.
