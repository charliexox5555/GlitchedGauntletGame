## Checkpoint

### Spindrift Vandal game fit

The owner approved the hero direction and asked for the harder facets and proportion cues of the stock Vandal. The fit pass is open in the live Blender session through port 9000.

- [Blender scene](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v02/Vandal_Spindrift_GameFit_v02.blend) · [Hero](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v02/Spindrift_Hero.png) · [Stock comparison](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v02/Spindrift_Fit_Comparison.png) · [Active prow](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v02/Spindrift_Crest.png) · [Fit checks](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v02/fit-check.json)
- Broader receiver and handguard flats, single-facet chamfers, a taller cutout stock, and a forward-curving magazine bring the profile closer to the stock Vandal. The split prow, stepped nacre fins, tidal seam, and keyed water release carry forward from the approved design.
- Measured grip and magazine sections are within 0.004 Blender units of stock. Five rays clear the stock aim center. The magazine follows the stock rig at nine sampled reload frames. For 302 archived hand poses, the largest increase in peak contact depth for either arm was 0.0006 Blender units. The model has 6,044 evaluated triangles excluding the water effects, against 5,171 for stock. Runtime testing is pending.

### Approved Spindrift Vandal concept

- [Blender scene](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/Vandal_Spindrift_Hero_v01.blend) · [Hero](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/Spindrift_Hero.png) · [Reverse](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/Spindrift_Reverse.png) · [Sightline](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/Spindrift_Sightline.png) · [Tide animation](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/Spindrift_Tide.webp) · [Checks](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Spindrift/Vandal/HeroApproval/v01/review.json)
- Authored in the interactive Blender session through MCP on port 9000. The new exterior has a lacquer crown, nacre crest plates, split prow, open stock, woven grip and an open crescent sight. The neck and trigger recess were revised after reviewing both sides. [Spindrift art direction](Spindrift/out/structural-v01/Vandal_concept_v01.png)
- Measured rest length is 3.896 Blender units against 3.899 for the stock Vandal. The stock aim center ray clears the new sight assembly. The meshes passed the closed-edge, face area and signed volume checks across 9,378 faces. The stock grip and aim anchors remain in the review scene. Hand clearance across stock reloads and runtime fit are pending.
- At frame 49 the prow flexes open as the water crests. Both settle by frame 82. The preview uses the model's keyed Blender geometry and curves.

### Earlier structural pass

24 / 24 structural files reopened. Targeted visual review is next.

The structural pass uses measured stock proportions and the existing motion cues. Spindrift gets swept pearl fins and a tidal seam. Kilnlock uses a slim graphite chassis, suspended spirit reactor, violet energy and warm metal indexing. Stacklight uses a stepped ivory bridge, open graphite frame and separate translucent cells. The scope covers offline Blender files. The rest and active beauty images are limited to the three heroes, three melees and three buddies.

The Kilnlock direction follows the dark alloys, power channels and warm hardware in the [owner's attached Prelude reference](Kilnlock/refs/Prelude_user_reference_2026-11-16.png). The Odin ceramic concept is retained in the art history.
