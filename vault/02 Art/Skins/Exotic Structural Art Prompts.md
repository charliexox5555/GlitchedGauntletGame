Main upper half: large dramatic left three-quarter at rest, muzzle left, clean slate backdrop, premium illustrated hard surface rendering, edges in silhouette. Bottom: a small exactly side-on rest profile, a compact centered first person view including new sight, and three same-angle closeup visual keyframes closed / fins open and flowing / locks flush. Constrain the body to a coherent nonfunctional fantasy art prop. Keep stock firearm internals and technical drafting out. No words, dimensions, firing, injury, copied logos or scene background.
