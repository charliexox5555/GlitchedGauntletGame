## Round two (2026-11-14, owner feedback: plates read as a reskin)

The owner rejected the plate-over-stock approach for this line and asked for the Moltframe full-sculpt vibe at the SilkLeaf quality bar, checked one Vandal at a time. **Vandal v02** (`tools/blender/model_giltfold_vandal.py` on the shared `model_vandal_shell_base.py`): the stock body is hidden and rebuilt as folded paper: one long climbing diagonal crease (ivory over slate) with a dark lacquer rail strip on top, a lapped paper blade, a single folded handguard plate with a wave-pattern window running into a lower spike, a two-plane stock with one big wave panel and an accordion-pleated butt, a full folded magazine with a lacquer strip and a boat base, a folded grip seated into the receiver, a twisted paper muzzle shroud and foil bar pairs. The wave pattern was rebuilt (staggered arches, fixed coordinate bug that drew full circles). Checks: 0 hits between any skin part and the bolt, charging handle, trigger or selector over Fire, Inspect, Reload and ReloadEmpty; the magazine catch meets the magazine back exactly where the stock catch meets the stock magazine; widths at stock (+6 % on the handguard); nothing floating. Owner notes on the first v02 draft (top folds unclear, stock pattern slivers, thin magazine, floating bits) are addressed in this build.

## Line language (as built)

Warm paper ivory planes (`#E8DAC0`, faint paper tooth) that meet a fold-shadow slate plane (`#86869A`) at one shallow diagonal crease per plate, ink lacquer recesses (`#26252D`) with an original champagne wave-scale pattern (Voronoi rings, upper halves only), pairs of short offset champagne foil bars seated on the plate surface (raycast), pewter metal. Static finishes throughout (the brief); the Fan carries the motion through its opening and closing. Plates sit over the stock geometry, so every moving part keeps its stock shape and travel. Kit: `tools/blender/model_giltfold_kit.py` (`fold_plate`, `foil_on`).
