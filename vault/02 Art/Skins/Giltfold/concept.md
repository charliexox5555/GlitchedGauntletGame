## Identity

Ivory panels that catch the light like folded card, with slate shadows, dark lacquer recesses, and small touches of champagne foil. On the guns, use two or three broad planes that meet at a shallow diagonal crease. The effect should read through the silhouette and the way the faces catch light. Save the obvious pleating for the fan. Use pairs of short offset foil bars as the original accent in the dark recesses.

The SilkLeaf references show long pale facets breaking over patterned dark insets and an open fan with a strongly pleated arc. Take that contrast and economy of shape into Gauntlet. Design original crease lines, trim placement, and fan graphics. [SilkLeaf collection reference](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/SilkLeaf_Collection)

## Fan poses and animation

This is the first folding fan in the Knife slot. Use the first-person project’s fixed-fist convention. Keep one primary grip at the bottom near the fan pivot. Animate the fan opening around that pivot while the grip stays seated in the hand. Use the same open and closed states for later fan cosmetics if their silhouettes allow it. [First-person melee animation notes](../First-Person%20Melee%20Animation.md)

| Beat | First-person pose brief |
| --- | --- |
| Draw | Pull the fan into view closed, spread it open in one deliberate motion, and settle into open idle. |
| Idle | Hold it open from the bottom, low on the right with the arc clear of the crosshair. |
| Light input | Swipe outward with the fan open, then settle back to the same idle pose. |
| Heavy input · right click | Fold the fan closed, play the forward heavy melee pose, then reopen on recovery. Keep existing melee gameplay and contact timing. |
| Inspect | Bring the fan up, show or reopen the leaf, make two small fanning motions toward the camera from the side of the view, and ease back to idle. |

Show the grip and pivot clearly in the open and closed views. During the later Studio prototype, check the transition from inspect to an input and the recovery to open idle. Check that ribs and leaf stay aligned as they close. The VALORANT Celestial Fan is a documented reference for self-fanning during inspect. A SilkLeaf showcase was located, but its playback could not be verified during this pass.
