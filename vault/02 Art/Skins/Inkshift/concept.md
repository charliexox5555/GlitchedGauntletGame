## Identity

Classic mecha armour drawn like a comic panel. Use broad warm-white plates, indigo-blue shadow faces, charcoal seams, sharp ink lines, and a few flat reflection shapes. The signature is a broken cyan accent in a dark recess, with two short marks offset from one longer segment. Use vermilion sparingly on small end plates and tabs. Put the strongest ink lines at the major panel boundaries. Let the smaller surfaces read through flat colour changes.

The Orion single-item images show clean inked panel breaks and hard highlight shapes. The lineup art shows a brighter cyan treatment. Use those two levels as a cue for the resting finish and optional motion study. Draw original panels and markings with no copied mobile-suit insignia or character motifs. Joseph Burgos credits Sean Bigham for the concept and describes his own linework and texture pass on the Odin, Phantom, red dot, bullets, and buddy mount. [Artist’s Orion linework notes](https://joseph-burgos.artstation.com/projects/1xk2lG?album_id=257009)

| Metal | Cool slate `#8796A8`, simplified hard highlights |
| Accent | Vermilion `#E46C40`, sparse |
| Neon | Electric cyan `#37E9FF`, brighter accents contained within the panel channels |
| Brass | Proposed cosmetic blue-grey casing with an orange stamp, using the stock game cartridge or shell form |
| Glass | Stock optic glass where present |

**Neon and motion.** Keep the cartoon finish legible even at rest. Give the cyan recess a steady low glow with one brief travelling glint across its three segments during the stock inspect as an optional prototype. Keep motion below the sightline and confined to the inset. Start from painted shadow shapes and baked ink lines so the look works with normal Studio lighting. Test the glint in the Gauntlet test range and use static cyan if it is distracting or the projection breaks up.

**Ammo and charm.** Try the blue-grey casing and orange stamp as an optional reload detail. Leave projectile behavior, stock shot effects, and ejection motion intact. Keep the buddy mount empty for this pass.

## Inkshift Blade pose study

Keep the hand planted at the same grip point. Set the idle low and off-centre. Adapt the stock Knife draw, light slash poses, heavy pose, and inspect to the longer blade’s screen coverage. Show the light slash silhouettes and a clear recovery back to idle. Show the heavy and inspect as separate poses. Verify that the tip stays readable, the guard clears the block fist, and the longer shape leaves room for the crosshair during idle and recovery. Use stock melee timing and gameplay in the prototype.
