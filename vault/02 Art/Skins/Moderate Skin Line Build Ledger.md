Bucky uses its stock model-sheet aim view for the sight comparison. For pistols without an ADS camera, the side-by-side sheet labels the fixed sight proxy. Operator retains the stock scope, lens geometry, materials, UVs and optical locators. The runtime scope overlay is outside the Blender pass.

The Blender pass reopened all assets and reimported every static FBX. Names, part separation, bone parents, triangle counts, pivots and the intended active UV channels survived. All four packed maps for every asset match the supplied PNGs. The 3,324 sampled stock action frames retained their original transforms, with no cosmetic intersections in the sampled stock and inspect sweeps. The Grade and Quality fields in each manifest record any small cosmetic silhouette differences, sampled sight results, and remaining geometry candidates.

- **Breakwater Bucky.** The orange trim belongs to the Pump bone and the stripe ends at the receiver. The amber tab flips on the fixed receiver. The right shoulder trim was removed after the early bolt sweep detected contact.
- **Sundial Guardian.** The porcelain shell stops at the stock barrel, and the brass dial is seated in a left-shoulder cut. The rotor travels 45° about its local normal. The dial bed and needle are visible in the rest and settled poses.
- **Sheriff.** The dial sits low on the right fixed frame to clear the stock reload sweep.
- **Playback regressions.** Bulldog uses [BoltFix v02](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Shared%20Sources/Bulldog/BoltFix_v02/Bulldog_RemasterV2_v05_BoltFix_v02.blend) and Stinger uses [HandguardFix v01](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Shared%20Sources/Stinger/HandguardFix_v01/Stinger_RemasterV2_v05_HandguardFix_v01.blend). See [Bulldog grazing and Fire](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Playback/Bulldog/v01/review/regression.jpg) and [Stinger left, right and underside](../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/Playback/Stinger/v01/review/regression.jpg).

For animation, enable the named cosmetic NLA tracks listed in each manifest. The stock actions are in the Blender file and the FBXs contain the static poses. Review the looks and hand the inspect references to the animator. Live hand clearance, lighting, sight fade and magazine visibility remain for the later Studio pass.
