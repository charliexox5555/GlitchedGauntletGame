# Three moderate skin line concepts

These working concepts are for shape-changing cosmetic variants of the existing Gauntlet guns. Each hero gets a remodelled outer shell, a distinctive surface treatment, and one short first-person inspect beat. Preserve the host's overall size, recognizable profile, hand contacts, stock ADS and moving-part clearances. Keep stock gameplay and combat timing. The rosters below are proposals for the first concept pass.

## Breakwater

**Fantasy** · Purpose-built gear from a storm rescue crew.

**Hero and proposed roster** · Bucky, Vandal, Spectre, Shorty.

- **Shell.** Rebuild the Bucky's pump exterior as a chamfered orange bumper around a charcoal grip surface. Give the fixed body a shorter, stepped teal shoulder and a contrasting off-white recovery stripe that ends at the moving joint. Repeat the orange bumper shape at a smaller scale on the Shorty.
- **Finish.** Satin signal-orange polymer `#F36A35`, deep enamel teal `#16575C`, worn off-white markings `#E4E3D8`, and dark rubber grip `#242C30`. Use light salt wear around exposed edges. Keep the big colour blocks clean.
- **Inspect.** The gun turns to show a small amber lock tab on a fixed side panel. The tab flips once and seats flush. Check the original pump grip and its full stock travel.
- **Reference lesson.** BlastX carries a product identity through proportions and material choices. For Breakwater the product is modern rescue equipment, with original shapes and markings. [Sean Bigham's BlastX development](https://bigham.artstation.com/projects/Krvm94)

## Playback

**Fantasy** · Late-1990s portable audio hardware.

**Hero and proposed roster** · Phantom, Bulldog, Stinger, Classic.

- **Shell.** Give the Phantom a long, low side recess with a smoked window, two offset faceted reels, and a band of brushed warm-grey metal. Give it a short rubberized end cap and an inset control strip. On the Classic, reduce the window to one visible reel and a tiny indicator. Keep the Phantom's stock suppressor and the Stinger's handguard contact area clear.
- **Finish.** Warm grey `#A49A8C`, dark smoke `#292A32`, aubergine `#49384C`, and muted lime indicators `#C1D870`. Use a fine directional brushing pattern and sparse printed marks on the inset.
- **Inspect.** As the player turns the gun, the reels advance under the window and a tiny level meter sweeps once. The mechanism is decorative and fixed to the body. A static smoked insert is the baseline.
- **Reference lesson.** Moltframe's exterior looks distinctive in silhouette and has one interior surface that rewards a closer look. Playback uses a rigid mechanism in a smaller window. [Moltframe review and fluid study](Moltframe/Build%20Ledger.md)

## Sundial

**Fantasy** · A precision instrument from an old observatory.

**Hero and proposed roster** · Guardian, Operator, Ghost, Sheriff.

- **Shell.** Sculpt the Guardian with a long chamfered porcelain spine, a tapered plum underside, and an octagonal brass dial recessed into the left shoulder. Let the crest and dial occupy most of the visual space and use a short, matching inlay on the stock. Scale the dial down for Ghost and keep the Sheriff's dial on the fixed frame.
- **Finish.** Bone porcelain `#E4D7BE`, deep plum `#3D2E42`, brushed brass `#B89A57`, and subdued pale-amber glass `#D8B37A`. Etch a few fine tick marks into the brass and keep the porcelain mostly quiet.
- **Inspect.** The inset dial turns one notch and pauses under an etched index mark. The dial turns inside its housing. Keep the optic housing separate, with its glass, reticle, and scope view at stock.
- **Reference lesson.** Origin makes a memorable inspect out of a real modeled ornament and reduces texture clutter where the shape already carries the design. Sundial uses a smaller, grounded motion. [Sean Bigham's Origin development](https://bigham.artstation.com/projects/R3G4ev)

## First concept pass

Start with Playback Phantom to establish how the window, frame and controls work at player-facing scale. For each hero, show a stock comparison, a three-quarter view, a left profile, a held view, a material close-up, and three inspect key poses. Compare fit and aim with the stock Blender model. Keep the appearance strong in the resting pose. [Gauntlet structural skin results](Structural%20Skin%20Round%20One%20Results.md) · [Riot's skin readability and animation guidance](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-craft-and-fantasy-of-valorant-weapon-skins/)

## Hero concept sheets

- **Playback Phantom.** Use the smoked reel window, shallow control strip, and muted lime meter as the line's signature. Match the stock suppressor profile, magazine placement, narrow stock neck, and iron-sight alignment in Blender. [Concept PNG](Playback/out/Phantom_concept_v02.png)
- **Breakwater Bucky.** Use the orange pump bumper, teal body, recovery stripe, and amber latch. The revised sheet restores the flush underbody. Check original pump travel and hand clearance with the orange bumper fitted. [Concept PNG](Breakwater/out/Bucky_concept_v02.png)
- **Sundial Guardian.** Use the porcelain spine and recessed brass dial as the line's signature. Match the stock exposed barrel length, rear sight height, and narrow stock neck. Check the dial against the stock aim view. [Concept PNG](Sundial/out/Guardian_concept_v02.png)

The sheets show design intent and inspect storyboards. Use the stock assets for dimensions, hand contacts, and aim alignment. Playback's later Bulldog and Stinger variants should carry forward the existing [Reskin Geometry Regression Checks](Reskin%20Geometry%20Regression%20Checks.md) for the Bulldog side panel and Stinger front grip.
