# Moderate skin line expansion

Add six firearm skins and one stock Knife variant for each of the three approved art directions. The [original batch](Moderate%20Skin%20Line%20Build%20Ledger.md) supplies the finish and inspect references.

| Line | New firearms | Knife |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Playback | **Ares** uses a long, two-reel tape deck below the feed cover. **Ghost** uses a single-reel cassette on the fixed frame and keeps the suppressor profile. | A recessed smoked reel and small lime level mark near the blade shoulder. Warm brushed blade flats, graphite contact pads and aubergine pommel. |
| Breakwater | **Judge** wears a compact orange fore-end bumper and a fixed receiver stripe. **Odin** carries a wider bumper and a teal rescue-hardware shell that clears the feed opening. | An orange guard surround, an off-white diagonal stripe on the fixed blade flat and a small amber tab at the guard. |
| Sundial | **Marshal** carries a porcelain spine and side dial below the optic. **Outlaw** puts the dial on the fixed frame clear of the break-action barrel. | A bone-coloured spine over plum blade flats and a recessed brass dial seated in the fixed guard. |

Start with the three knives to establish their scale and separation. Use the [stock Knife model sheet](../../../art-source/weapons/ModelSheets/Knife_model_sheet.png) and RemasterV2 v05. Keep the grip contact, blade locators, stock attack timing and reach, and draw, slash, heavy and inspect clips. Put cosmetic moving parts on their own pivots and keep a baked static finish.

Build the six firearms from their own v05 sources. Keep moving parts and contact geometry clear, with extra checks around the Ares and Odin feeds, the Outlaw barrel hinge, and the Marshal scope. Reopen each blend, reimport the local FBX, and capture matched stock views and inspect poses. Save a manifest with the four baked maps and Blender-only evidence. Studio, uploads and game integration remain deferred.
