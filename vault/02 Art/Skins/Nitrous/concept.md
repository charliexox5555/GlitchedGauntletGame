## Pattern and details

- **Pattern or motif:** Carry broad, hard-edged geometric blocks from front to rear across Body and Panel. Use black as the dominant field, brushed silver as open space between clusters, and electric blue (#2367F2) as the main painted colour. Concentrate hot orange (#FF631F) at the burst and echo it in a few sharp, solid paint flecks. Keep the Grip and Dark roles near-black.
- **Signature detail (proposed):** On the left receiver, a compact orange splash breaks into a few angular flecks between outward blue wedges and a brushed-silver cutout.
- **Alignment:** Where a shape crosses adjacent fixed panels, align the angle, width and colour across the seam. End shapes cleanly at moving or removable parts. Keep the same landmarks in the left, right, top and three-quarter views so the layout reads as one surface treatment.
- **Shape:** Preserve the stock silhouette, proportions and part positions. Keep the stylised low-poly forms, flat colour areas and clean edges.
- **Add-on pieces:** None.
- **Matching charm:** None.

## Do not

- Avoid uneven paint edges, colour bleed, drifting shapes between views, broken alignment at fixed seams, noisy speckling, photoreal wear, small text, real brand logos and copied skin layouts.
- Keep custom animation and special visual effects out of this reskin pass.
