## Identity

Flight hardware for a fictional Gauntlet space mission. Use satin white enamel over gently bevelled service panels, black insulation and grip areas, one inset of champagne-gold thermal foil, and small mission-blue fittings. The left-side signature is a rectangular gold inset with one clipped corner, paired with two short blue alignment marks on the surrounding white panel. Suggest folded foil with a few broad highlights. Keep markings sparse and original.

The Endeavour references show how the same aerospace palette stretches from a compact Ghost to an Operator. Keep the clean hierarchy of white housings, dark working surfaces, warm foil, and small blue details. Invent Gauntlet’s panel layouts and service marks. [Endeavour collection](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Endeavour_Collection)
