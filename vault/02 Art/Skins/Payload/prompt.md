Read [concept.md](concept.md) and inspect all four style references. Generate an individual light-grey design sheet per gun. Start with Bulldog, then Ghost, Vandal, Operator, Ares, Odin, Classic, and Stinger.

> Draw an original Payload cosmetic for the fictional Roblox game Glitched Gauntlet. Use the selected host gun's stock sheet for silhouette, hand contacts, sight alignment, moving-part layout, and camera views. Use the Endeavour images for colour and aerospace hardware references. Create original Gauntlet service panels and markings.
>
> Show broad satin flight-white equipment panels over cool grey and graphite, one recessed champagne thermal-foil insert with a clipped corner, and two short mission-blue marks. Limit the foil pattern to a few broad glints. Make the blue fittings legible without added glow. Use the per-item placements in the concept brief. Include a large front-left three-quarter view, left and top views, first-person and applicable stock ADS crops, and labelled material swatches. Include the item's nominated check view from the brief. Keep static textures, stock ammunition, and stock animation and shot presentation. Omit agency logos, borrowed decals, a starfield backdrop, and melee variants.
