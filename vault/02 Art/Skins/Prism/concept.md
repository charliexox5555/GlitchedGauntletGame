## Pattern and details

- **Pattern or motif:** Carry the palette across each weapon in broad, part-sized colour blocks. Give Body large glossy magenta faces and Panel deep plum inset faces. Keep Grip and Dark near-black. Use the existing planes to break up the reflections. Use solid colour blocks with clean, stylised reflections.
- **Signature detail:** One long, pale-pink specular highlight across the left Body face that breaks at an existing panel seam. This is a proposed treatment for the player-facing view.
- **Shape:** Match the stock silhouette, proportions and part positions. Minor surface refinements may follow existing planes and bevels within the stock outline. Keep the sight picture and functional parts clear.
- **Add-on pieces:** None.
- **Matching charm:** None.
- **Knife:** Carry the magenta and plum finish onto the existing blade faces and keep the handle near-black. Use the basic knife sheet for the outline and part positions.

## Do not

- Dull or muddy pink, busy decals, photoreal wear, small text on the weapon, real brand logos and copied skin layouts.
- Custom reload or inspect animations and special visual effects are outside this first pass.
