## Pattern and details

- **Pattern or motif:** Run a broad charcoal-to-crimson gradient across most of the Body shell, with the darkest area toward the front and muted crimson toward the rear. Carry the colour shift into adjoining Panel faces in darker oxblood tones. Layer fine matte grain under sparse, broken drafting lines and short registration ticks on Body and Panel. Cluster the markings near selected seams and leave large clear areas so they read at first-person scale. Keep the magazine, grip and other Dark parts matte black.
- **Signature detail (proposed):** A thin muted-red drafting line on the left side takes one right-angle step at an existing panel break and ends in two short perpendicular ticks.
- **Shape:** Keep the stock silhouette, proportions and part positions. Apply the finish and markings as surface treatments within the existing part boundaries.
- **Add-on pieces:** None proposed for this reskin.
- **Matching charm:** None.
- **Knife:** Apply the gradient and stepped drafting line to the player-facing blade face of the stock Knife, scaling the motif to fit. Keep the handle matte black and preserve the blade profile.

## Do not

- Avoid bright scarlet, glowing perimeter lines, a uniform grid, a flat recolour without drafting detail, busy markings, photoreal wear, tiny text, real brand logos, and copied skin layouts.
- Keep custom reloads, inspect animations and special visual effects out of this pass.
