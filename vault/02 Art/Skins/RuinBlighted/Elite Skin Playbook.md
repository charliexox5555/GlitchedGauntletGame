# Elite skin playbook

The verified reference is Shadow Mist Vandal v05 and its November 18 Match playtest. Use its workflow for the Ruin-Blighted expansion and later elite skins. Start with the host's stock Blender file, rather than transferring Vandal geometry or copying its position constants. The Vandal measurements below are review evidence for that one skin. [Blender review](Build%20Review.md) · [Idle and ADS playtest](../../../05%20Production/Playtests/2026-11-17%20Shadow%20Mist%20Idle.md) · [Action and audio playtest](../../../05%20Production/Playtests/2026-11-18%20Shadow%20Mist%20Action%20Polish.md)

## Approval fixture

Spawn the approved hero in Match before judging the candidate. Capture the normal hold, full ADS, and at least one action with effects visible. Use the actual installed variant's name and asset ID. Compare side by side at the same viewport size. For Ruin-Blighted use Shadow Mist Vandal v05 and its shader datablocks, export roles, seam placement, and effect sockets. Fit the authored planes and breaks to the candidate rig and use cam-facing projection to match the aperture's apparent size. Preserve the host barrel profile unless the owner asks to change it. The next gun goes to review after the owner approves the first adaptation. [Guardian v10 example](Expansion%20Ledger.md#guardian-v10-approval-pass)

## Blender and export

1. Freeze the stock source version and use its rig, locator, animation, and camera contract. Preserve grip contact, engagement timing, and which assemblies move. Block the fore-end, receiver, stock and magazine from their actual side, rear, underside, held, and aim views. In a scoped host, verify the optical axis and scope hiding. The Vandal's final direction traded the crown for a clean cleaved spine and eight independent rear-sight fragments. Retain a host's actual sight picture when adapting that idea.
2. Seat large faceted armor to the shell, use smaller closed fragments where the breaks need depth, and carry a few tapered seafoam veins across the build. Keep grip fabric dark and its shape close to stock. Binding geometry on a magazine follows the magazine; optional fixed-body inlays follow their owning bone. Use separately named origins for intentional floating pieces. Short local rays, a grazing light, and one-sided rendering catch hidden issues from both sides.
3. Export with preserve-by-bone grouping, named `*Sight*` meshes, deliberate material groups, and a manifest of mesh rest transforms, rigid joints, phases, movement axes, emitters, and glow groups. Temporary export bones are created in a separate process and should leave the source rig intact. The Vandal exporter currently embeds Vandal joint, sight center, and emitter coordinates. Measure each host before generalizing those values. Share the same emitter and region vocabulary. [Flat exporter](../../../../tools/blender/export_roblox_flat.py) · [Vandal exporter](../../../../tools/blender/export_ruin_blighted.py) · [Vandal v05 manifest](../../../../art-source/weapons/Skins/RuinBlighted/Vandal/v05/manifest_flat.json)
4. Reopen the versioned blend and check stock assets against the saved result, then inspect the reimport. Scrub normal and empty reload, draw, inspect and fire, with extra frames near contact events. Sweep the authored idle movement over a full cycle and repeat sight checks with the stock camera. Preserve the stock front-post or optic reference. Check hand proxies if available and use actual R15 hands in Studio before calling a pose verified. Vandal v05 passed a 48-phase shard sweep and both stock aim cameras. Its 9,802 visible triangles and 133 MeshParts were acceptable for the local art preview, with device performance still open.

## Animation, effects and Studio

1. Connect to the verified Match place and preserve existing working changes. Stage a versioned model with the flat installer, confirm all installed meshes, names, attachments and motors, and retain a local backup. Keep owner-facing review in the preview system until release. Use the actual Match play camera for aim, interruption and playback evidence. [InstallFlatSkin](../../../../tools/studio/InstallFlatSkin.luau) · [Known failure checks](../Skin%20Build%20Pitfalls.md)
2. Use the host's actual ready, ammo, seat and rack markers. For Vandal, the mag starts releasing at about 0.37 s on the tactical clip, forms clearly in the support hand, seats at about 1.58 s, and leaves the remaining duration for the authored finish. Empty uses different timing. Drive hand motion, shard pose, brightness and sound on the same action clock. Each adaptation needs host-specific staging and tests. When inspect is interrupted, return to the held pose and suppress its lock impact. If shooting interrupts, time the shot to land on the forward-facing pose. [Action logic](../../../../src/client/Util/ShadowMistActions.luau) · [Action effects](../../../../src/client/Util/ShadowMistActionFx.luau)
3. Keep idle mist narrow and low contrast. Let local light and slow regional movement hold the visual through rest and a spray. Use phased group pulses across the stock, receiver and fore-end. Taper smoke and movement at ADS and keep the aperture clear. Suppress visibility and emission for a released or hidden magazine. Keep flash, tracer wake and wall scorch brief, independent and removable. A per-skin setup should maintain presentation on stock fallback, spectator/replay, and equipment switch. [Idle controller](../../../../src/client/Util/ShadowMistIdle.luau) · [Skin settings](../../../../src/shared/Config/SkinPreview.luau)
4. Design sound around the actual motion markers. Reuse the Vandal's elemental layers and distinctive lock where actions share a sound, then pick a stock or newly sourced report appropriate to each host. A family resemblance should leave the cadence, suppression, and report of each host recognizable. Keep a cue sheet with file stems, new takes, reuse notes, spatial mix and interruptible beds. [Vandal sound cue sheet](Sound%20Cue%20Sheet.md)

## Known regressions to retest

- A large moving decoration can cut into aim even when static side views look clear. Check both aim cameras and restore the stock visible sight reference.
- Flat export grouping can lose the sight name, causing runtime ADS fade to hide it. Inspect the actual installed sight names and the rendered view.
- Stock mag and scope visibility must also control skin meshes and their visual layers. Compare reload and scope in the live viewmodel as well as Blender.
- Front fragments can pass through the support hand at a different shard phase. Seat them further from the hand or move their idle axis along the body.
- Animation events must reset on early ADS, shot interrupt, unequip, and death. Audio and particles should follow the cleanup. Reproduce normal and empty reload at 1× before signing off.

## Worked expansion

Use the [Ruin-Blighted expansion ledger](Expansion%20Ledger.md) for the four stock-rig adaptations and the independently rigged Reaver. [The Blender builder](../../../../tools/blender/build_ruin_blighted_expansion.py) shows how armor can fit the magazine's own cross sections and how to export animatable fragments for each host. The Studio controller tests measured cast travel in both reload routes and used the actual preview model attributes to identify the correct clone. The [sound queue](Expansion%20Sound%20Queue.md) lists the reusable source takes and the dedicated follow-up cues.

For the rebuild, identify complete stock rail islands and remove them before sculpting the spine. Replace the visible action mesh on its existing joint and keep the grip and optic locators. Store the new shard joints in the saved Blender master as well as the import manifest. In Studio, test the real auto-empty reload by firing the magazine dry. An isolated reload animation confirms pose geometry while the live input route confirms readiness and foley.
