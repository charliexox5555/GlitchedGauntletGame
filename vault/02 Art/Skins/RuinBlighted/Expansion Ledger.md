## Verified

- Reopened all four v05 Blender masters. Each retains the original tactical and empty reload actions and carries its newly articulated meshes on the saved rig. The Guardian, Spectre and Operator center rays clear the new geometry. Measured rail removals were 28, 13 and 15 linked islands respectively. The Ghost has no ADS in the stock game.
- Match Studio retained all stock motor offsets and aiming, grip, muzzle and ejection locators. The Operator retains the stock scope lens. All six variants are installed under `ReplicatedStorage.WeaponSkins.RuinBlighted` in place `133387545285290`.
- Normal-speed Play Solo inputs exercised draw, firing, tactical reload, actual empty reload and inspect for each rebuilt gun. Maximum mag travel in viewmodel studs was 0.566 Guardian, 0.618 Spectre, 0.534 Operator and 0.745 Ghost in both reload routes. The cast ribbon lit during the throw, each inspect played one lock impact, and the shooting pulse reached 4.6–4.7 brightness. Aim engaged on Guardian, Spectre and Operator.
- The v04 Reaver baseline used eight blade joints and ten actions. The Solo input check exercised draw, all three light swings, heavy, idle tip plant and inspect. Four spectral links followed the blade. Inspect separated the shards by up to 0.324 studs before its single lock impact at 3.25 s.
- StyLua and Selene passed. Match and Lobby both built with Rojo.

## Final Blender MCP review and Reaver v05

Opened the five saved gun masters and their stock counterparts in the live Blender MCP session on port 9876. Across 279 magazine samples from the original tactical and empty actions, the position delta was zero for every v05 gun. Stock bone matrices and locators also matched. The new geometry cleared the center aim ray on Vandal, Guardian, Spectre and Operator. The Ghost has no stock ADS fixture.

The Reaver rebuild keeps its 4.815-stud overall length and slimmed the blade edge from 0.278 to 0.118 studs. Guard span went from 1.461 to 0.869 studs and grip thickness from 0.204 to 0.138 studs. Individual blade profiles now taper into a single silhouette with a narrower luminous fracture. The v05 master was reopened in the live Blender MCP session with all eight blade joints and ten actions present. The previous v04 model remains available for rollback.

The shared gun polish adds a moving highlight along the magazine throw ribbon, including a narrower version on Vandal, and a slightly brighter shot pulse in the shard seams. In Match Studio Solo the highlight traveled across the ribbon and cleared after seating on all five guns. The Reaver's new pose brings the bevel into view, with animated wisps, narrower shard links and a shorter swing trail. I checked the held pose in the actual Match viewport and exercised draw, all three light swings, heavy and inspect. The final draw played one lock at about 0.72 seconds. Inspect separated the blade by 0.274 studs, then played one impact with the blade back at rest. Match and Lobby built successfully, and StyLua and Selene passed. Match Studio was left in Solo with the Reaver equipped.
