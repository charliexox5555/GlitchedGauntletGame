## Model direction

Sculpt a faceted dark slate and obsidian shell around the Vandal's compact dark-metal core. Concentrate the biggest broken plates at the handguard and rear stock, with a few smaller fragments framing the gaps. The player-facing side should show the fractures in ordinary light. Preserve the stock grip envelope and functional landmarks so the result still reads as a Vandal in the held view.

The owner removed the crown in v02. Keep the eight fractured rear-sight segments around the stock aiming center and preserve the front post. The outer handguard and stock shards can sit slightly off the frame so the gaps read in the held view.

Retain the curved magazine envelope, grip area, travel, and original pivot. Give its outer shell the larger fracture cuts from the sheet. Use an opaque, subdued teal material ID on the binding surfaces and in selected seam recesses so the geometry reads in a plain render. Preserve the stock charm mount location. The custom round and Blighted Crown Charm are separate assets in the full breakdown.

The base is charcoal and obsidian with spectral teal and seafoam energy. The owner asked for a distinct upper profile, so a cleaved obsidian spine replaces the ribbed rail and a recessed shard shutter replaces the exposed stock action. Give both sides and the underside their own fragments, with the larger masses close to the silhouette.

Carry the seafoam inlays and local light from the handguard across the receiver, magazine, buffer and heel. Keep the light largely steady and place a thin stream of translucent smoke at the breaks in the shell. Let it drift and fade close to the gun. Hold light through aim and inspect. Reduce the body shard travel during ADS, keep the eight fractured rear-sight pieces breathing outward from the stock aiming center, and clear the airborne smoke from the sight window.

## First-person action effects

Draw presents the exposed frame, winds seafoam around the stock and fore-end, then gathers the plates in staggered order and locks on a low thud. Firing adds a short black-and-teal flame, a tapered dart with a fading mist wake, and a light pulse and flex through the armor. Concrete and other solid walls take a dark scorch that wisps for 1.5 seconds. On reload, cut the magazine bindings as the old shell drops and fades. Stage the new shell out in the support hand, then throw it into the visible magwell and flash the binding lights on seating. The 5.4-second inspect lifts and opens the rifle, holds the lit moving plates through a second tilt, and closes them into the settled pose. Fade the inspect before a queued shot so the flash lands on the forward-facing rifle.

Void Wraith, Ashen Wyrm, and Blighted Emerald remain documented in the owner breakdown for a later color pass. The owner sheet illustrates the material and shape direction. Use the stock Blender source for dimensions, pose, and camera alignment.
