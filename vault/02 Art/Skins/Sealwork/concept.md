## Identity

Carved black-jade surfaces with warm gold fittings and deep vermilion inlays. Give the black faces soft, rounded highlights like polished stone. Frame one small red seal near the player-facing side of the body with a stepped gold setting. Use a clipped-corner seal with an original offset groove. Carry the groove and paired gold borders through the line. Keep large areas of dark material so the gold breaks read at first-person size.

The Ruin images use black surfaces, ornate gold borders, and jewel-like red insets. Take the material contrast and the distinction between broad panels and small ornaments. Use original seal shapes and framing, with no borrowed symbols or ornament layouts. [Ruin collection reference](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Ruin_Collection)
