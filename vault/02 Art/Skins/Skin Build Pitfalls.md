# Skin build pitfalls (and the check that catches each one)

Every problem the owner found in the structural skins (round two, 2026-11-14), what caused it, the fix, and the check that now catches it. **Run the checks in the last section before showing the owner any skin.** Results of the passes: [Structural Skin Round One Results](Structural%20Skin%20Round%20One%20Results.md).

## Geometry

| Problem (owner's words) | Cause | Fix | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moltframe Vandal, Spectre, Ares: "gap at the back of the gun" | The lower stock strut's front end stopped 0.008-0.05 studs short of the receiver (built to a point behind the rear cowl). | Strut start moved along its own line into the receiver core (Vandal `p0` -.135/.254; Spectre -.26/.3465; Ares -.13/.341). All 8 front corners now test inside the receiver. | Magenta backdrop capture of the stock from the side; Blender: the strut's front verts tested inside a closed receiver mesh (ray parity). |
| Moltframe Dagger: "gap between the blade and handle", "the spines look weird" | `blade_station()` fell through to the **tip** height for x before its first station, so the blade's base ring (x .355) collapsed to a sliver at the tip height: a thin fin poked out of the guard top and the full-width blade only started in front of the guard. The guard's upper horn arched over the spine and left an open slot; a thin separate spine fang read as a stray shard. | Station lookup holds the first value below the first station; horn underside rests on the spine; fang removed (the horn is the one spike). | Magenta backdrop behind the knife; any helper that interpolates a profile must clamp **both** ends. |
| Inkshift Blade: "the tip looks weird, there's no sharp edge" | The black ink outline (inverted hull) is pushed out by the same amount everywhere; at a point the averaged normals fan out and the hull rounds the tip into a dark blob. | `ink_outline(..., taper=)`: the blade's hull thins to 6% over the last 0.2 studs. | Any inverted hull on a point or blade edge gets a taper; close-up of the tip. |
| Inkshift Odin: "glitched part" (orange plate); Payload Bulldog: dotted line by the bolt slot | **Coplanar faces (z-fighting).** Two layer plates at the same lift (stripe and shadow both +0.004 on the armour), a bolt face flush with the receiver, pinstripes and crowns laid at lift 0, retained stock hardware with two flush materials. | `export_roblox_flat.resolve_coplanar()` lifts one side of every visible same-facing overlap by 0.004 studs at export (LihFolk: Roblox suggests 0.01 for world parts; 0.004 is invisible on a gun and holds to about 70 studs) (accent over body, skin plate over stock hardware, else the smaller face). The lifts are listed in `manifest_flat.json` → `coplanar_lifted`. Found and fixed on 24 of 31 items. | Built into every export. Builders: never give two layers the same `lift`; nothing at lift 0 on a surface. |
| Found in the Blender final check (not yet seen by the owner, since the hand covers it): grips half see-through with black triangles (Inkshift Odin, Phantom, Shorty; Payload Ares, Bulldog, Odin; also the Ghost frame, Odin receiver piece and Vandal grip outlines) | **Blender 5.2's `mathutils.geometry.convex_hull_2d` is unreliable** on stock meshes with near-duplicate points: on the Odin grip it returned the first corner twice and dropped a real one. `hull_xz()` built side outlines from it, so the outline cut a diagonal through the part: half the side face missing, half flipped. | `model_vandal_shell_base.convex_hull_xz()` (monotone chain) replaces it; the 9 affected items were rebuilt. | Never use `convex_hull_2d`. | Found in the Blender final check: a see-through strip between the Sealwork Sheriff's grip and frame | The skin grip is the stock grip's outline with a 0.007 chamfer; along the frame's diagonal underside the chamfer pulled the grip's top edge away from the frame. | The grip's top-edge corners moved into the frame (0.006 past contact). | Same as the strut rule: a part that meets another is buried in it, checked with the culled magenta render. |
| (round 1) "parts look see-through / hollow" | Usually async loading in the capture, not geometry. Real case: open plates facing inward. | `fix_outward` + `orient_open_islands` at export. | Preload before capturing; raycast a suspected hole (memory `studio-capture-preload`). |

## Studio and code

| Problem | Cause | Fix | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Sights disappear on ADS" (Moltframe Spectre, Giltfold and Inkshift Stinger, Inkshift and Payload Odin, Sealwork Guardian, Payload Ares) | `ViewmodelController.findOccluders` fades every part on the eye-to-AimPoint line **unless its name contains "Sight"**. The flat export merged the retained `<Gun>_Sights` hardware into one MeshPart per material (`SK_Root_NN`), so the rear sight lost its name and faded on aim, with the rest of that material. | Export keeps every object named `*Sight*` in its own parts, `SK_<Bone>_Sight_NN`. | The ADS sight-line simulation (below) on every skinned gun: 0 non-sight parts hit. |
| Operator: "when zoomed in you can still see the texture of the part in front"; "on reloads you can see the texture of the mags" | A part's `LocalTransparencyModifier` (or `Transparency`) does **not** hide its Textures/Decals. The scope hides the gun, reloads hide the mag, but the skins' tiled pattern Textures stayed. | `client/Util/LocalHide.set(part, value)` hides a part and its Textures/Decals; used by every viewmodel hide (scope, mags, carried rounds, props, ADS fade, held Mods, the third-person gun in first person). `MagDrop` fades the dropped copy's Textures with it. | Any new code that hides a part uses `LocalHide`; grep `LocalTransparencyModifier` in viewmodel code. |

**Left as the stock guns have them** (also see-through in the stock models, so they are design, not skin faults): the Bulldog's sling slot through the stock (the Payload strap half covers it), the Guardian's rail bridging the gas block, the Sheriff's cylinder gap in the frame window, the Classic's grip-to-magazine seam.

## Rules for new skins (from the above)
1. **Sights:** name sight geometry `*Sight*` (skin-built sights too), and keep the sight line clear of anything else.
2. **Layers:** every decorative layer gets its own lift, at least 0.004 apart; none at 0, none sharing a lift with an overlapping layer.
3. **Joins:** struts, stocks and blades end **inside** the part they join (bury 0.02+), never at its surface or short of it.
4. **Profiles:** helpers that interpolate a profile clamp at both ends.
5. **Outlines:** inverted hulls taper to almost nothing at points and blade edges.
6. **Textures:** any code that hides or fades a part must also hide its Textures (`LocalHide`).

## Checks to run (scripts)
- **ADS sight line** (Studio, Edit, MCP `execute_luau`): clone each `WeaponSkins/<Line>/<Gun>` into a WorldModel at viewmodel scale 0.8, cast the five `findOccluders` rays from the eye (AimPoint + adsDistance) to the AimPoint, list hit parts without "Sight" in the name. Expect none.
- **Flush faces:** `resolve_coplanar(objs, dry=True)` after an export pass returns `[]`.
- **Gaps:** magenta Neon backdrop behind the model, side captures of every joint (stock to receiver, blade to guard, grip to frame). In Blender: the end of every strut or blade tested inside the part it joins.
- **Textures hide:** in play, aim a scoped skin and reload a skinned gun; nothing of the gun shows in the scope and the old mag's pattern leaves with it.
- **Crossed faces:** for every polygon with 4+ corners, project to its plane and test its outline for self-crossing (`ngon` audit, scratch script pattern in `fix_crossed_faces`). Expect none in the export.
- **What Studio gets (the final gate):** re-import each exported `*_RobloxFlat.fbx` into an empty Blender scene, colour by the manifest, render both sides and a rear three-quarter in Workbench with **backface culling on and a magenta background**. Any magenta inside the silhouette is a hole Studio will show; black triangles are flipped faces. Normal Blender renders hide both (they draw back faces).
