# Slipframe draft image prompt

Use after all six round-one briefs have been discussed. Read [concept.md](concept.md) and the reference images before drafting.

## Guardian hero

> Create a light-grey technical concept sheet for Slipframe, an original Gauntlet cosmetic variant of the Guardian. Use the stock Guardian sheet for its dimensions, camera angles, grip placement, and sight alignment. Use the attached Intergrade images for cool blue-grey contrast, long bevels, and layered industrial forms. Create original shapes and inlay placement.
>
> Render a smooth satin titanium shell with a broad face of smoky anodised blue around a charcoal inner frame. Keep the surfaces clean and opaque. Place one narrow, recessed silver-blue inlay on the player-facing side and stop it at a solid grey break in the shell. Include a large front-left three-quarter view, side and top views, first-person and stock ADS crops, and labelled material swatches. Include a reload detail showing the grey cartridge and its dark-blue band, along with three small close-ups of the same inlay showing rest, the passing glint, and rest again during the stock inspect. Keep the shape consistent through the inlay study. Keep the rest of the shell quiet and omit large glow outlines, dense circuitry, floating parts, and effects around the gun.

## Supporting guns and dagger

> Use the reviewed Slipframe Guardian sheet as a material and shape-language anchor. Make individual sheets for Operator, Classic, Spectre, and Vandal using each gun’s stock sheet for silhouette, grip placement, moving parts, and aim view. Keep the Spectre’s suppressor profile readable and the Vandal’s curved magazine and stock identifiable. Use the per-item shell and inlay placement in the concept brief. For the dagger, show an original compact asymmetrical wedge blade with a brushed silver face, polished cobalt bevel, graphite spine, and dark metal grip. Include a profile, grip-side close-up, and first-person idle and inspect using the stock Knife hand posture as the starting point.
