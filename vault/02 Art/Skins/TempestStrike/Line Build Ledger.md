# Tempest-Strike line: build ledger (Stinger, Marshal, Bucky, Sheriff, Kunai-Katana)

Blender-only build of the rest of the Tempest-Strike line, per the [Line Blender Handoff](Line%20Blender%20Handoff.md). One section per item: stock contract, every version with its tri count, grade and review sheet, then the export. The Studio port and VFX are the Vandal session's (not here).

**Setup.** Live Blender 5.2 on port 9999 (pid 35100, owner-confirmed 2026-11-21). Shared line tools in `tools/blender/tempest_line/` (`ts_lib.py` helpers copied and adapted from the Vandal's `tv_s_lib.py`/`tv_lib.py`; `20_render.py` views, `30_sheet.py` save + sheet, `32_grid.py` close-ups, `46_ads.py` ADS camera + five findOccluders rays, `49_audit.py` geometry audit, `53_export_prep.py` export copy + `TS_Rig`, `60_export_flat.py` flat exporter + one-sided check; send with `run.sh <script> K=V ...`). Per-gun build stages in `tools/blender/tempest_<gun>/`. Review sheets and close-ups in [out/](out/).

**Notes and skills used.** `gauntlet-vault`, `gauntlet-build-style` (no round curves, carved ends, every join has a transition), `blender-director` + `hard-surface` (primary forms, panel breaks, boolean cuts, separate moving parts), [concept brief](concept.md), [Vandal Hero Plan](Vandal%20Hero%20Plan.md) (r01–s08 lessons: build on stock, not a reskin, crisp flat shading, plates thickened outward, buried joins), [Skin Build Pitfalls](../Skin%20Build%20Pitfalls.md) (sights named `*Sight*`, separate lifts, joins buried 0.02+, one-sided check), `src/shared/Config/Weapons.luau` (ADS distance and FOV per gun).

**Line rules applied to every item.** Stock `RemasterV2 v05` opened fresh and restyled on top (stock file never saved); replaced stock parts hidden with `ts_replaced`. Flat shading, at most one 0.001 chamfer; near-square octagons (diagonals ≤ 16 %). Silver plates seated in cut recesses (dark floor) and standing 0.0035 proud, or cut from the part's own surface and thickened outward. Leather wraps are tilted loft bands over a dark core (outer edge 0.0018 fuller, so each band reads as a wrapped layer), cyan conduits recessed between them. Storm Shadow palette = the Vandal's final s05 values. Grade = lowest rubric area.
