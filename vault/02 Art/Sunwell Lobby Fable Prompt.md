## Build the main hall

1. **Greybox and walk it first.** Enter on center from a shallow ramp into a tall rectangular concourse. Start around 68 × 112 studs with a 24-stud clear center walk. From spawn looking forward, put **DUELS near left**, **LOCKER near right**, **RANGE farther left** and **SHOP farther right**. Start with 16 studs clear at each opening. Verify R15 scale, the first view, sign reads and walking lines before fixing dimensions.
2. **Balance the architecture.** Keep the main roof ribs, wall bays, central approach, paired bench recesses and guardrails aligned around the centerline. Mount a still, chunky turbine into the far wall with one bronze Gauntlet crest and a low rail. Leave the middle free for movement. Give the four entrances individual inner frames, materials, task lighting and floor treatments.
3. **Make the signs architectural.** Put actual depth, backing and mounts in the lettering. Center `SUNWELL WORKS` in the entrance lintel as chunky cast-style letters. Use leaning athletic `DUELS` in a chamfered blast frame with one orange wedge bracket at each end and two short orange guide inlays running into the opening, perpendicular to the sill. Use upright squared `LOCKER` on a gently arched plate, a ribbed steel collar and a cool-blue sill line. Fit stenciled `RANGE` into a shutter head above mint calibration ticks. Set broad `SHOP` letters into a low ribbed valance with vertical amber end caps and a short dark entry mat. Test the exact words at player distance and on phone. Keep lighting secondary to readable silhouettes. Use directional marks where the route continues, including at the reverse view of the spawn landing.
4. **Make one strong bay and bench before repeating.** Use broad flat faces, matte color blocks and visible small bevels. Start around 0.30–0.45 studs on large concrete edges and 0.04–0.10 on prop edges, usually one or two segments. Reserve larger curves for true forms like the Locker collar and the bench bend. Model the bench as one formed graphite shell with two inset olive-slate pads, two steel sleds and seated rubber feet. Start at 6.5 studs wide, 2.25 deep and 1.7 at the seat. Check the side, back and underside. Use the same cast concrete, charcoal, petrol steel, shallow seams and mounted opal lighting throughout. Concentrate wear at contact points and check the result at ordinary Roblox graphics settings.
