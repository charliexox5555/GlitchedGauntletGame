- The Mod models imported turned (none of the gun axis presets fit): built with a new axes option; reported to the Blender session.
- An action with a touch binding fires `Pressed` twice: `InputController` filters duplicate edges.
- Enemy cooldowns were readable and SLOWED tags showed through walls: both fixed.
