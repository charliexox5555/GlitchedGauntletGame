# Experience naming review

2026-11-19. The owner reviewed the longlist below. **Draft Duels** was the strongest response. **Loadout Duels** and **Take Your Pick** had elements the owner liked.

## After the owner's review

Use short, natural names that suggest an FPS with powers you choose. The owner described the ability draft as the game identity to bring forward. They rejected run-and-gun as a description, as well as names that lead with weapon tiers, round count, a venue, or a long phrase. Avoid a leading “The.” Match mechanics and the bunker were directions from the earlier exploration.

| Name | What it communicates |
| --- | --- |
| Draft Duels | Picking before a matchup. The current benchmark and the owner's strongest reaction so far. |
| Power Duels | Powers in small competitive matches. |
| Guns & Powers | The clearest literal combination of guns and active abilities. |
| Draft & Fire | The draft and then the shooting. |
| Mod Duels | Uses the game's in-world ability name. Can suggest community modding to a new player. |
| Power Draft | Makes choosing abilities explicit. |
| Pick & Fire | A shorter take on the part the owner liked in Take Your Pick. |
| Draft Shootout | Combines drafting and the gunfight in the title. |

First-page results from Roblox's public experience search contained no matching phrase for these eight queries, including both spellings of Guns & Powers and spelling out “and” for Draft & Fire and Pick & Fire. Web research found two close titles. A web game uses the singular Draft Duel, and Roblox has Knife Ability Duels. This is a preliminary collision check. ## Research

- Deagle Arena and Sniper Duels name what players use. Gunfight Arena names the activity and accommodates a broader weapon roster. [Deagle Arena](https://www.roblox.com/games/84556640895285/Deagle-Arena) · [Sniper Duels](https://www.roblox.com/games/109397169461300/SNIPER-DUELS) · [Gunfight Arena](https://www.roblox.com/games/11879883939/Gunfight-Arena)
- RIVALS names the relationship, while Arsenal evokes its weapon variety. Hypershot hints at pace and gunplay. Ragdoll Soccer and Blade Ball pair recognizable ideas to give other PvP games an immediate image. [RIVALS](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/RIVALS) · [Arsenal](https://www.roblox.com/games/286090429/Arsenal) · [Hypershot](https://www.roblox.com/games/17516596118/Hypershot) · [Ragdoll Soccer](https://www.roblox.com/games/106931373443671/Ragdoll-Soccer) · [Blade Ball](https://www.roblox.com/games/13772394625/Blade-Ball)
- ROUNDS is the closest comparison for between-round upgrades. Friends vs Friends is an FPS with deck building that puts the social matchup in the name. These samples illustrate naming strategies and do not isolate the effect of a title on discovery. [ROUNDS](https://landfall.se/rounds) · [Friends vs Friends](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1785150/Friends_vs_Friends/) · [Roblox Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)

## Theme explored in the first pass

First-to-five gun duels. Start with sidearms, pick or upgrade abilities between rounds, and get access to heavier weapons as the match progresses. The bunker, range of maps and duel pads suggest an organized competition. The darker facility look is a naming option alongside the game loop. The older all-digital world premise retired in the Game Plan. [Match Flow](../01%20Design/Match%20Flow.md) · [Lobby V4 Theme](../02%20Art/Lobby%20V4%20Theme.md) · [Game Plan](Game%20Plan.md)
