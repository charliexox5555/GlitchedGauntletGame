# Experience naming and brand direction

Updated on 2026-11-19. The owner rejected Stackfire, Go Loud, Dead Even and Heat Check. See the [Experience Naming Review](Experience%20Naming%20Review.md) for the active longlist.

## Earlier spoken-name pass

The owner wants a name that sounds good when a friend says “Wanna play some ___?” Their examples include RIVALS, Blade Ball, Deagle Duels and Adopt Me. A familiar word or phrase works here. The key art and the first description line can explain the gun choices and the Mod draft. [RIVALS](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/RIVALS) · [Blade Ball](https://www.roblox.com/games/13772394625/Blade-Ball) · [Adopt Me](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237/Adopt-Me)

| Name | Read aloud | Impression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Go Loud** | “Hop on Go Loud.” | Familiar phrase with an aggressive shooter read. Can also suggest stealth or heists. |
| **Dead Even** | “You down for some Dead Even?” | Suggests a close matchup. The idea fits both teams getting the same gun choices each round. The art needs to show the FPS part. |
| **Heat Check** | “Wanna play some Heat Check?” | Sounds like a challenge you issue after a win. Strong basketball association. |

**Go Loud** is the current recommendation to try aloud. Suggested first description line: “Fast 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 FPS duels. Pick a gun and draft or upgrade an ability every round.”

The owner's **Loaded** and **Loaded Up** have the everyday phrasing this pass is after. Roblox search surfaced **LOADED Duels**, and **Loaded Up** is already used by an indie game. The first page of Roblox's public experience search returned no exact title for the three current candidates. This is a collision spot check. [LOADED Duels](https://www.roblox.com/games/134813444098365/LOADED-Duels) · [Loaded Up](https://mauseri.itch.io/loaded-up)

## What the name should promise

The launch game is a fast 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 FPS. Each round opens with a gun choice and a choice to draft or upgrade a Mod. Both sides get the same weapon tier and their Mod builds grow over the match. The title should carry that mix of gunplay and escalation across future modes. Glitch can name rare upgrades, and Gauntlet can name the future event once it exists. See [Match Flow](../01%20Design/Match%20Flow.md), the [Developer Handoff](../04%20Tech/Developer%20Handoff.md) and the active [Match config](../../src/shared/Config/Match.luau).

## What comparable names do

- **Weapon plus format.** Knife Duels, Sniper Duels and Deagle Arena give a clear fantasy immediately because the weapon is the premise. [Knife Duels](https://www.roblox.com/games/79877593860968/KNIFE-DUELS) · [Sniper Duels](https://www.roblox.com/games/109397169461300/SNIPER-DUELS) · [Deagle Arena](https://www.roblox.com/games/84556640895285/Deagle-Arena)
- **Brand plus description.** RIVALS names the relationship and spells out FPS duels and team sizes in the first lines. RIOTFALL uses a short compound that can carry multiple guns and formats. [RIVALS](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/RIVALS) · [RIOTFALL](https://www.roblox.com/games/7796842481/RIOTFALL)
- **Genre plus venue.** Gunfight Arena has immediate genre clarity. A similarly broad title would make the round-to-round build harder to remember. [Gunfight Arena](https://www.roblox.com/games/11879883939/Gunfight-Arena)

Roblox Creator Hub advises original naming and imagery, and thumbnails that represent what players actually get. These listings illustrate different ways to communicate a premise. They provide no comparison of title performance for the same game. ## Archived Stackfire concept

- **Display name** **STACKFIRE**. Read and write it as Stackfire in conversation.
- **Tagline** **Build your kit. Win the round.**
- **Roblox description opening** “Fast 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 FPS duels. Pick your gun and draft or upgrade an ability every round. Both teams get access to stronger weapons as the match goes on.”
- **Visual identity** A compact uppercase wordmark beside three rising, cut-corner plates. Use near-black graphite, warm white and the existing signal gold `#FFC83D`. Apply the graphic to the loading lockup and one Lobby V4 sign when the title is approved. Use existing team colors for gameplay information. [Style Bible](../02%20Art/Style%20Bible.md) · [Lobby V4 Theme](../02%20Art/Lobby%20V4%20Theme.md)
- **Roblox icon** Test the three-plate mark on graphite next to a close first-person crop of a real match at 48–64 px. Judge brand recognition and shooter recognition separately.
- **First thumbnail** Capture a slide around a real Frost Wall on a shipped map such as Rooftops, with an opponent legible across the lane. Camera and weapon silhouette should read before the copy. Keep the wordmark at the top and essential content away from the bottom metadata area.
- **Second thumbnail or short trailer beat** Show a Mod pick, that same Mod in play, and a cut from sidearm to later weapon tier. Show why the next round feels different within the first few seconds.

## Earlier decision test

The owner rejected Stackfire after trying it in conversation. For the current shortlist, say the names aloud first. If more than one survives, put them on the same gameplay crop with the same descriptor, then ask a few players what they expect to play and how they would type each title after hearing it once.
