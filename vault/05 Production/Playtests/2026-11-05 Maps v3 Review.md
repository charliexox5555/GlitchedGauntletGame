# Maps v3 review (2026-11-05, late evening)

The user played Rooftops2 v3 (7.92) and Dockyard2 v3 (6.67) with ForceMap. Notes: [Rooftops2](../../01%20Design/Maps/Rooftops2.md), [Dockyard2](../../01%20Design/Maps/Dockyard2.md).

## Verdict
Better than v2, but **"some things are built not well"**. The build quality still isn't at the concepts' level. The problem is the modelling, not only the textures: **too many simple shapes**.

### Rooftops2: still the weak point
- **Text and signs** look bad: blocky and amateur.
- **The wheelbarrow in the middle** looks bad.
- **The cacti** look unprofessional.
- **Scale:** some things are not to scale with the player.
- **Arrows** are not aligned with the things they point at.
- **Some buildings** don't look professional.
- **Cohesion:** the pieces don't share one vibe or one level of finish and skill.
- The reviewers' points are confirmed as well: the AC stacks block routes, the Pit is dark, the roof floors are big flat fields, and the quoins are oversized.

### Dockyard2: much better, still has issues
- **A bit dark.**
- **The crate textures** could be better.
- **The "SHED 02" text** is blocky and too simple.
- Simple shapes in places.

## Decisions
- **Maps first.** The avatar holds wait; the user will look at them later.
- **Rebuild the weak pieces as real models** based on the Codex concepts: modelled in Blender, with a silhouette and bevels, not stacked parts.
- **Then Codex builds props too**, through its Blender MCP. The core chat drives the Codex app (see [Codex Collaboration](../../04%20Tech/Codex%20Collaboration.md)).
- **No blocky part-built text.** Signage is a decal or texture or a modelled mesh, or it's cut.
- **Scale check against the R15 player for every prop.**
