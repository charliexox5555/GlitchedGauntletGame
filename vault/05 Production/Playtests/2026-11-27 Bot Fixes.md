# Bot fixes (2026-11-27)

Workstream 2 of the [Launch Hardening plan](../Launch%20Hardening%202026-11-27.md). A regular player's report: "half the bots are glitched sometimes and they don't turn and don't move well". Design: [Bots](../../01%20Design/Bots.md). Code: `src/server/Services/BotService.luau`, `src/server/Bots/Nav.luau`, `src/shared/Config/Bots.luau` (`WATCHDOG`, `STAIRS`).

## Causes found

| # | Cause | How it was found | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clients took over bot bodies.** Roblox resets a pinned network owner to automatic whenever the assembly changes. One welded Part is enough (measured in Studio: `SetNetworkOwner(nil)`, weld a Part, `GetNetworkOwnershipAuto()` is true again and the owner is the player). Every gun equip welds the world gun on, and a held Mod welds its prop, so right after spawn the nearest player's client owned the bot. The server's `Humanoid:Move` and `AlignOrientation` then stopped reaching it: no turning, bad movement, worst for the bots near players ("half the bots"). | First 1v1 run: the live bot's owner was the player, auto ownership on. | `BotService.pinOwner`: re-pin after any part or joint is added to or removed from the bot (once per frame at most), and on every watchdog sample (counted as `OwnerDrift`). Commit `1868e6b7`. |
| 2 | **Bots could not climb Part stairs.** Rooftops' sunken middle court (floor 6 studs below the upper level) is reached by flights of 0.75-stud steps. `Nav.canMove` casts a body box whose bottom sits 1.7 studs above the feet, 4 studs ahead. On those stairs the treads 4 studs ahead are 2.4 studs up, so every heading up the stairs read as blocked, and the floor check refused any rise over 2 studs. A bot that walked into the court could not leave; it stood at the steps for the rest of the round. | Another session's hosted 2v2 on Rooftops4: both enemy bots logged `bot stuck … goal=Roam owner=server` every 6.5 s from about 15 s into each round. Probing from where they stood showed `Step` / `StepCheekSlope` hits ahead. | A rise that climbs evenly (each half of the look-ahead at most half-length × 0.75 + 0.3 studs, so a sheer ledge still fails) is walkable when the body box, raised to ride the steps, clears the flight (`Config/Bots STAIRS`). Commit `bcf99c8e`. The first version still failed in Studio: the box reaches half its depth (1.7 studs) past the look-ahead point, where the flight is higher than the floor sample (measured at the stuck bot: rise 1.95 at 4 studs, cast hit at 2.5). The raise now comes from the floor under the box's front edge and the box faces the heading. Commit `cd2c0d4f`. |
| 3 | **One erroring bot stopped the rest.** `BotService.step` ran every bot's think and act unprotected in one Heartbeat loop, so a bot raising an error every frame froze every bot after it in the list. | Code read. | Think, act and the watchdog run under `xpcall`. A failing bot drops its route and inputs and tries again next frame. Errors are logged at most every 10 s per bot (`[Health] … bot error`, Analytics `BotError`). |
| 4 | **The shared Path lock could stick.** All bots share one Path object behind a `busy` flag. A compute that never returned would have stopped every bot's routing. | Code read. | Token-based lock with a 5 s timeout; the compute runs under `pcall`. |

Ruled out: bots spawning before the map is ready (`MapService.load` registers the map's bounds before any spawn) and state left over between rounds (`spawnBot` resets the route, cover, aim and inputs each life). Not seen: a broken aim spring (no `StuckTurn` trip in any run; the watchdog would re-seat it).

Also noticed: Rooftops' map centre (the middle patrol point) sits inside the Pump House. The route there fails and the patrol skips it, which works but costs a few seconds in a centre-lane opening.

## The watchdog

`BotService` samples each live, fighting bot every 0.5 s. It trips when the bot asks to move (eased steering input over 0.3, not slowed below 0.35 by a Mod) but covers under 2 studs in 4 s, or when the body's yaw lags the aim spring by over 35° for 1.5 s. Recovery: re-pin the body to the server, drop the route and cover plan (the stalled patrol point is skipped for 9 s), re-seat the aim on the body, re-enable the turn constraint and get the humanoid out of any odd state. No teleports. Each trip prints a `[Health] S bot stuck` line (goal, owner, state, walk speed, position, steering, path) and sends Analytics `BotStuck` live. Studio counters on `ServerStorage.BotOwners`: `StuckMove`, `StuckTurn`, `OwnerDrift`, `BotErrors`.

## Range bots idle (routed from loading-perf)

`Range/RangeBots` stepped its strafers, carriages and damage boards every frame on every server, reserved match servers included. code-audit's first gate (`aa3b6e9e`) paused them unless someone was in the Lobby or the Range. Now:
- No Heartbeat connection at all unless a player is in the Range zone. It connects when the first player walks in and disconnects when the last one leaves (`PlayZone.changed`, `PlayerRemoving`), stopping each strafer's `Move` so they hold their lanes.
- A reserved (hosted) server doesn't set the range bots up until its first Range visit (hosted servers become lobbies after their match).
- Range only, not Lobby: the range sits about 4,180 studs from the hub (bots around z 2583, the hub around z −1597) and is reached through a full-screen transition, so nobody in the lobby can see it.

Studio, Lobby server: in the lobby 0 of 41 range bots moved; after `RangeTravel` into the Range the 4 strafers moved; after leaving, 0 again. Hosted 1v1 mid-match: 0 of 41 range bots set up (`NameDisplayDistance` still at its default).
